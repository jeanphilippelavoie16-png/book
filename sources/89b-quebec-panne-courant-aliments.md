## Extraits intégraux, tels que relevés le 2026-09-23

> « frozen foods can be stored for 48 hours in a properly functioning freezer that is filled to
> capacity »

> « In a half-filled freezer, the storage time is 24 hours. »

> « food stored in it will stay cold for about four to six hours after a power outage »
> (réfrigérateur)

> « Partially thawed food with frost on the surface of the package: can be refrozen if the centre
> of the food is still hard. »

> « Thawed raw foods that have remained at 4 °C or below: can be cooked immediately for quick
> consumption or refrozen after cooking. »

> « Completely thawed perishable foods: discard if exposed to temperatures above 4 °C. »

> « When in doubt, it's best to throw them away. »
