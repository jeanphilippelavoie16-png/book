## 1. *WHO recommendations for the prevention and treatment of postpartum haemorrhage* (2012)

- **URL :** https://iris.who.int/handle/10665/75411 (fichier :
  https://iris.who.int/server/api/core/bitstreams/36ea885b-7472-4b89-9605-81535f6a53c6/content)
- **Copie :** `29-femmes-oms-hemorragie-post-partum-2012.pdf`
- Définition (p. 1) : « Postpartum Haemorrhage (PPH) is commonly defined as a blood loss of **500 ml
  or more within 24 hours after birth**. » « **Most deaths resulting from PPH occur during the first
  24 hours after birth** ».
- Recommandation 3 : sans ocytocine, « oral misoprostol (**600 µg**) is recommended ».
- Recommandation 4 : « In settings where skilled birth attendants are not present and oxytocin is
  unavailable, the administration of misoprostol (600 µg PO) by community health care workers and lay
  health workers is recommended for the prevention of PPH. »
- **Recommandation 6** : « In settings where skilled birth attendants are unavailable, CCT [traction
  contrôlée du cordon] is not recommended. »
- Recommandation 7 : « Late cord clamping (performed after **1 to 3 minutes** after birth) ».
- Recommandation 10 : évaluer le tonus de l'utérus chez toutes les femmes après la naissance.
- Recommandation 14 : en traitement, « sublingual misoprostol, **800 µg** ».
- Remarque (p. 15) : « If PPH prophylaxis with misoprostol has been administered and if injectable
  uterotonics are unavailable, there is **insufficient evidence to guide further misoprostol dosing**
  and consideration must be given to the risk of potential toxicity. » Préoccupation de
  « hyperpyrexia » à 800 µg. « the potential for serious consequences of administration before
  birth ».
- Recommandation 17 : « Uterine massage is recommended for the treatment of PPH. »
- Recommandations 21 et 22 : « bimanual uterine compression » et « external aortic compression [...]
  as a temporizing measure until appropriate care is available ».
- Recommandation 24 : « The use of uterine packing is not recommended » pour l'atonie.

## 2. *WHO recommendations: intrapartum care for a positive childbirth experience* (2018)

- **URL :** https://iris.who.int/handle/10665/260178 (fichier :
  https://iris.who.int/server/api/core/bitstreams/ba043cf7-cba4-484d-bf7e-ec79c4102d54/content)
- **Copie :** `29-femmes-oms-soins-intrapartum-2018.pdf`
- Recommandation 6 : « a standard duration of the latent first stage has not been established » ;
  « the duration of active first stage (from 5 cm until full cervical dilatation) usually does not
  extend beyond **12 hours in first labours**, and usually does not extend beyond **10 hours in
  subsequent labours**. » Remarque : « The **median** duration of active first stage is **4 hours** in
  first labours and **3 hours** in second and subsequent labours ».
- Recommandation 33 : « In first labours, birth is usually completed within **3 hours** whereas in
  subsequent labours, birth is usually completed within **2 hours**. »
- Recommandation 48 : peau à peau pendant la première heure « to prevent hypothermia and promote
  breastfeeding ».
- Recommandation 49 : mettre au sein « as soon as possible after birth ».
- Recommandation 50 : « All newborns should be given 1 mg of vitamin K intramuscularly after birth ».
- Recommandation 51 : bain retardé de 24 heures ; « one to two layers of clothes more than adults,
  and use of hats/caps ».
- Recommandation 55 : surveiller saignement, contraction de l'utérus, hauteur du fond, température
  et pouls pendant les 24 premières heures ; « Urine void should be documented within six hours. »
