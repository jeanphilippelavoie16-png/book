## Extraits intégraux

> « The Eastern population comprises approximately 6800 individuals, which is equivalent to
> 2100 pairs. »

> « most individuals (> 95% of the population) winter in the St. Lawrence Estuary and Gulf »

> « Reason for Designation: Numbers of individuals in this eastern population are limited. »

> « hunters often have difficulty distinguishing between the two species from a distance.
> Consequently, Barrow's Goldeneyes are at risk of being inadvertently shot by hunters of the
> Common Goldeneye »

> « The behaviour of the Barrow's Goldeneye also makes it vulnerable to hunting mortality since
> it is easily attracted by decoys. »
