## Extraits intégraux — seuils et cinétique

> « scurvy usually occurs at a plasma concentration of less than 11 µmol/L (0.2 mg/dL) »

> « A total body pool of less than 300 mg is associated with scurvy symptoms »

> « the amount needed to prevent scorbutic gingivitis (less than 10 mg/day) »

> « Catabolic turnover varies widely, about 10 to 45 mg/day, over a wide range of dietary
> intakes due to body pool size. »

> « maximum body pools are limited to about 2 g »

> « the biological half-life of ascorbate varies widely from 8 to 40 days and is inversely
> related to the ascorbate body pool »

Fumeurs : « metabolic turnover of the vitamin in smokers averaged about double that of
nonsmokers: 70.0 versus 35.7 mg/day ».

## Extrait intégral — signes cliniques

> « follicular hyperkeratosis, petechiae, ecchymoses, coiled hairs, inflamed and bleeding
> gums, perifollicular hemorrhages, joint effusions, arthralgia, and impaired wound healing »

Autres signes relevés sur la page : dyspnée, œdème, faiblesse musculaire. Chez des sujets
carencés **sans scorbut déclaré**, « gingival inflammation » et la fatigue étaient les
marqueurs les plus sensibles de la déficience. Chez le nourrisson, la carence peut causer des
anomalies osseuses et des symptômes hémorragiques.

## Extrait intégral — absorption

> « Some 70 to 90 percent of usual dietary intakes of ascorbic acid (30 to 180 mg/day) are
> absorbed, although absorption decreases to about 50 percent and less with single doses
> above 1 g. »

> « Intestinal absorption of ascorbic acid occurs through a sodium-dependent active transport
> process that is saturable and dose dependent. »

> « The type of food consumed has not been shown to have a significant effect on absorption
> of either intrinsic or supplemental vitamin C. »

> « The bioavailabilities of the vitamin from foods and supplements are not significantly
> different. »
