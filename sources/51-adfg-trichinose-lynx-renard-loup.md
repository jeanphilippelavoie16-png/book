## Extraits intégraux

> « Trichinosis from Alaskan wildlife is caused by a roundworm called Trichinella nativa. »

> « Trichinosis occurs in bears (black, grizzly and polar bears), wolves, foxes (arctic and red),
> wolverine, lynx, walruses, seals, and ground squirrels. »

> « Trichinosis is hard to detect when butchering because there are few signs. »

> « All bear and lynx meat should be considered possibly infected. »

> « Meat should be well cooked (internal temperature of meat should be at least 160° F). »

> « Freezing meat does NOT kill the Trichnella species found in Alaska's wildlife. »

> « Smoking, drying, salting or microwaving do not always kill the parasites, only proper cooking
> or canning is known to make the meat safe to eat. »

## Lecture

160 °F = 71 °C, seuil de l'Alaska ; le livre garde **74 °C**, qui le couvre. Trois des cinq bêtes
du chapitre 51 sont nommées : **loup, renard roux, lynx** — et le lynx est mis au même rang que
l'ours.
