## Règles (*Changes in Your Body*, *Abnormal Menstrual Bleeding*)

- « You can buy or make sanitary pads of folded cloth or wads of cotton to catch the blood [...]
  They stay in place with a belt or by attaching to your underwear. Pads should be changed several
  times each day, and washed well with soap and water if they are to be used again. »
- Règles abondantes : « The menstrual flow soaks through your pad, cloth, or tampon every hour for
  several hours in a row » ; « Menstrual periods last more than a week » ; « Blood clots (dark red
  shiny lumps) larger than 2½ cm (1 inch) ». « having them often can lead to anemia. »
- « Menstrual periods usually come about every 21 to 35 days ». Causes des règles qui cessent :
  grossesse, fausse couche, « not getting enough healthy food to eat », entre autres.

## Méthodes sans stock (*Behavioral Methods of Family Planning*)

- Allaitement : prévient la grossesse « only when all 3 of these things are true ».
- Méthodes d'observation : « will also not work if your menstrual cycle varies a lot [...] or has
  recently been disrupted » — dont l'allaitement. « Use both the Mucus Method and the Standard Days
  Method at the same time. »
- Méthode de la glaire : vérifier chaque après-midi ou soir, « in your underwear, or by wiping the
  outside of your vagina with your finger, a piece of paper, or cloth » ; toute glaire = fertile ce
  jour-là et le lendemain ; « Do not douche or wash out your vagina at any time. This will wash the
  mucus away. »
- Collier : « On the first day of your period, put a ring or string around the red bead. Each day,
  move the ring past one bead. When the ring is on any of the white beads, you may get pregnant ».
- Retrait : « Sometimes the person does not withdraw in time. Or sometimes, a little semen can leak
  out of the penis before ejaculation. »
- **Ce qui ne marche pas :** « Omens and magic » ; « Putting grasses, leaves, pods, and dung in the
  vagina can cause infection and irritation » ; « Washing out the vagina (douching) with herbs,
  powders, cola drinks or other liquids does not prevent pregnancy. Sperm move very fast and some
  will reach the inside of the womb before they can be washed out » ; « Urinating after sex does not
  prevent pregnancy. »

## Après la naissance (*Care after the birth*)

- « Feel the top of the womb. It should be hard and rounded, at about the level of the navel. If
  the womb feels soft, ask the person to pass urine, then rub the womb ».
- Yeux du bébé : « wash out the eyes with breast milk or cool, boiled water and a clean cloth. »
- Cordon : garder propre et sec (Hesperian dit « clean it with alcohol » ; **écart retenu** :
  l'INSPQ 2025 dit de ne pas employer d'alcool, qui retarde la chute — le livre suit l'INSPQ).
- « To prevent infection, they should not have sex or put anything in their vagina until their
  bleeding stops. » « They should get a lot of rest for at least 6 weeks. » « do not sit in water
  until a full week passes after the birth. » « needs to eat more food than usual. »
- Déchirure : linge chaud et humide ; « pouring water over their genitals while passing urine may
  help. » « Do not put plant medicines inside the vagina. »
- Saignement dangereux : « soaks more than 2 pads or cloths in an hour, the day after birth » ;
  « a small flow of bleeding that does not stop in the days or weeks after birth » ; « foul-smelling
  bleeding, fever ». « Bleeding that starts more than a day after the birth is most often caused by
  pieces of placenta left in the womb or womb infection. »
- Infection de l'utérus : fièvre et frissons, douleur et sensibilité du ventre, « bad-smelling fluid
  from the vagina ».
- Tétanos du nouveau-né : fièvre, ne tète pas, pleure tout le temps, respiration rapide, corps qui
  raidit.
