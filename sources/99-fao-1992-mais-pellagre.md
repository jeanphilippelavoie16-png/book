## Extraits intégraux

> « The water-soluble vitamin nicotinic acid has attracted much research because of its association
> with niacin deficiency or pellagra, which is prevalent in populations consuming high amounts of
> maize »

> « The association of maize intake and pellagra is a result of the low levels of niacin in the
> grain, although experimental evidence has shown that amino acid imbalances, such as the ratio of
> leucine to isoleucine, and the availability of tryptophan are also important »

> « The alkaline treatment of maize has been reported to destroy its pellagragenic factor. »

> « This point has been extensively debated between those who claim that niacin in maize is bound
> and not available to the animal and those who favour the theory of improved amino acid balance
> induced by the alkaline-cooking process »

## Ce que le livre en tire

Une phrase, sans chiffre : là où le maïs devient la base de la table, la pellagre suit ; la cuisson
alcaline améliore le grain, et le mécanisme exact reste débattu. Le chapitre ne dit pas « la
lessive libère la niacine » comme un fait établi : il dit qu'elle améliore la valeur du grain
(Mt. Pleasant 2016 : « increases calcium, protein quality, and niacin ») et que la pellagre suit le
maïs mangé seul. Sur la Côte-Nord, le maïs ne sera jamais la base : c'est une note, pas un danger.
