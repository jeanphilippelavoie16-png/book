## Extrait (question 2, « Does a change in color indicate spoilage? »)

> « Change in color alone does not mean the product is spoiled. Color changes are normal for
> fresh product. With spoilage there can be a change in color—often a fading or darkening. In
> addition to the color change, the meat or poultry will have an off odor, be sticky or tacky to
> the touch, or it may be slimy. If meat has developed these characteristics, it should not be
> used. »

| Élément de la note | Appui |
