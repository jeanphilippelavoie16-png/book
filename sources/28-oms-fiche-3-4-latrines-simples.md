## Extraits intégraux (transcrits)

**Page 17 :**

> « It consists of a square, rectangular or circular pit dug into the ground, covered by a hygienic
> cover slab or floor, with a hole through which excreta fall into the pit. Depending on user
> preference, a seat or squat hole with footrests can be installed, and a lid supplied to cover the
> hole. »

> « Simple pit latrines can, however, produce unpleasant odours and allow flies to breed easily. »

**Page 26 — « Routine maintenance » :**

> « Keep the latrine floor clean by cleaning daily using water or ashes. »

> « Household waste, such as vegetable or fruit peel, and organic matter, such as sawdust and leaves,
> may be placed in the latrine and can help to reduce odours, but will decrease the life of the pit. »

> « When the contents reach a level of 0.5 metres from the surface, then the contents of the pit
> should be covered over with soil and a new pit should be dug. »

> « To stop mosquitoes breeding in pits, pits should be kept as dry as possible. If too much water
> enters, ashes or dry horse or cow dung thrown in helps to absorb water and odours. »

> « No disinfectant should be added to the pit. »

**Page 28 :**

> « The VIP latrine should not have a lid over the squat hole, which must be left open to allow air to
> flow into the pit. »

## Ce que la fiche établit

- La sciure et les feuilles jetées dans la fosse **réduisent l'odeur** mais **remplissent la fosse plus
  vite** ; la cendre absorbe l'eau et l'odeur.
- La latrine **simple** a un couvercle ; la latrine **ventilée (VIP)** n'en a pas.
- Aucun désinfectant dans la fosse.

## Ce qu'elle ne dit pas

- **Aucun volume de matériau de couverture** par personne ou par usage.
