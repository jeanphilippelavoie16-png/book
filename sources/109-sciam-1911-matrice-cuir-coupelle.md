## Extrait intégral

> « MEN who have much work to do around hydraulic presses know how annoying it is to find they are
> out of cup leathers just when they are in a hurry for one. […] The iron dies generally used to
> make cup leathers are also rather expensive. At one time it was part of the writer's work to keep
> several hydraulic presses in order. These presses worked under a pressure of 4,000 pounds to the
> square inch. The pistons were between 12 and 14 inches in diameter and over a foot long (solid
> iron), and leather cups were used on them. A set of dies was made out of hard wood. The
> illustration shows a sectional view of them. They were turned out on a lathe. The concave part
> was turned out to the size of the cylinder in which the piston worked, and the convex part was
> turned down to a diameter one-half inch smaller. The leather was cut to a diameter twice that of
> the piston plus twice the depth of the depression in the die. It was moistened in tepid water for
> a few minutes, and then pressed in the dies. With a heavy weight to provide the necessary
> compression the leather was left for several hours. A single pair of dies was used for two
> years, at the end of which time they were apparently as good as ever. »

## Ce que la planche en prend

- Le godet se forme **entre deux pièces de bois tournées** : un trou (la partie concave) au
  diamètre du cylindre, et un mandrin (la partie convexe) plus petit de deux épaisseurs de cuir.
  Le chapitre dit « former à chaud sur un mandrin de bois tourné au diamètre du piston » : la
  planche montre le mandrin enfoncé dans un trou percé au diamètre du cylindre, le cuir pressé
  entre les deux.
- Aucun chiffre ne passe dans la planche ni dans le chapitre. Le cuir de la source est mouillé à
  l'eau tiède ; celui du chapitre est trempé au suif chaud : la planche suit le chapitre.
- La phrase sur le diamètre de la rondelle (« twice that of the piston ») est ambiguë dans la
  source ; le chapitre dit « au diamètre du piston plus la hauteur du rebord ». Rien n'est coté.
