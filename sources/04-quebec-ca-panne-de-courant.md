## Extraits intégraux utilisés

### Trousse et préparation

> "flashlight and spare batteries; battery-powered radio; candles; camp stove and the
> recommended fuel; lighter or matches; warm blankets"

> Install carbon monoxide detectors if using wood-burning fireplaces or gas heaters.
> Have non-electric heating systems inspected and cleaned annually by qualified
> professionals.

### Pendant la panne

> "Stop or turn off all electric appliances that were running when the power went out
> (stove, dishwasher, kettle, washer, dryer, etc.)"

> "Unplug all electronic devices (computer, television, etc.); this will avoid the
> devices being affected by a voltage surge"

> Do not unplug refrigerators/freezers; limit opening doors.
> Food in refrigerators stays cold "about 4 to 6 hours".
> Frozen food in well-filled freezers can stay preserved "up to 48 hours".
> Leave one light on per floor to detect power restoration.

### Génératrice

> "Make sure your generator is installed outdoors (i.e., not in your home nor in your
> garage), in a properly ventilated area far away from doors and windows"

> "Before starting your generator, set your home's main switch to OFF"

> Connect devices "directly into the generator's outlets"

> "you should usually shut it off for one (1) hour every eight (8) hours"

> Cool generator before refueling.

### Monoxyde de carbone

> Never use combustible appliances indoors (propane BBQs, charcoal grills, camp stoves).
> If detector sounds: "Leave your home immediately. Call 911"

### Plomberie, panne d'hiver

> Close leader inlet.
> "Purge the faucets and pipes"
> "Pour antifreeze in the toilets (bowl and tank) as well as in sink and bathtub traps"

### Aliments et médicaments

> After 6+ hours: check food condition before eating.
> Avoid taking refrigerated medications if storage was impossible.

### Après le rétablissement

> "Open the leader inlet as well as all the faucets so that any trapped air can escape"

> If evacuated: ensure water heater is full before restoring main electrical panel.
> Have an expert restore gas service (do not do it yourself).
> Gradually restore heating equipment.

### Eau de puits après un sinistre

> Have water analyzed by certified laboratory.
> Boil water "for at least one minute" until standards confirmed.
> Flush pipes "for at least five (5) minutes" after confirmation.
