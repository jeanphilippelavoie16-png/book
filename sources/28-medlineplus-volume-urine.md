## Extrait intégral

> « The normal range for 24-hour urine volume is 800 to 2,000 milliliters per day (with a normal fluid
> intake of about 2 liters per day). »

> « Disorders that cause reduced urine volume include dehydration, not enough fluid intake, or some
> types of chronic kidney disease. »
