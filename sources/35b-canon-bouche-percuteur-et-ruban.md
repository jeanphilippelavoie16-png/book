# Canon bouché, percuteur tenu en avant, ruban sur la bouche

- **Chapitre qui s'en sert :** 35b (§3, §5-C étape 12, §5-G étapes 36 à 38 et note 1, §7, §8 blocs
  1 à 3)
- **Date de consultation :** 2026-09-24 (pages téléchargées, texte extrait)
- **Langue :** anglais
- **Nature :** aucune page officielle canadienne consultée ne décrit la vérification d'un canon selon
  le type d'arme. Les pages retenues sont des publications de la NRA (association américaine), un
  guide d'étude de chasseur approuvé par l'IHEA (association internationale des éducateurs en
  sécurité à la chasse), Wikipédia et une revue de chasse. **Aucune n'est officielle** ; la nature
  de chacune est dite ci-dessous.

---

## 1. NRA Family — « Gun Safety Tips: Bore Obstructions »

- **Auteur :** NRA Staff, 8 janvier 2025
- **URL :** https://www.nrafamily.org/content/gun-safety-tips-bore-obstructions/

> « Any object in the bore that impedes the progress of the bullet toward the muzzle has the
> potential of raising pressures to a hazardous level. There are many types of objects that can
> serve as bore obstructions: cleaning patches, snow, mud, dirt or other material that may be
> collected from the environment; even bullets from a squib load or portions of a bullet jacket. »

> « An obstruction in the middle or forward part of the barrel may cause a ring bulge or, in some
> cases, bursting of the barrel at that point. [...] Cases have been seen in which a separated
> bullet jacket in the bore caused a small ring bulge and was ironed into the bore at that point,
> allowing the passage of subsequent bullets without other signs noticeable by the shooter. »

> « In this scenario, the 20-gauge shell not only acts as a bore obstruction, but itself often fires
> when hit by the 12-gauge load. »

> « In the field, keep the gun's muzzle away from snow, water and mud. At all times, if a squib load
> is suspected, unload the firearm, open the action and ensure the bore is clear, either by visual
> inspection (from the breech end!) or by running a cleaning rod the length of the bore. »

## 2. Be a Safe Hunter — « Firearm Obstructions »

- **Éditeur :** E.S.N.N.A. Inc., guide d'étude de cours de chasseur, « IHEA approved » selon le site
- **URL :** https://beasafehunter.org/firearm-obstructions

> « Point the barrel of the firearm in a safe direction
> Open the action
> Make sure the firearm is unloaded
> Look for debris in the barrel. (If at all possible, do this from the action end by removing the
> bolt or hinging the firearm. If this is not possible, use a light to illuminate the inside and
> look down the barrel.)
> Using a cleaning rod remove any obstruction
> Check the barrel again, to ensure it remains free of debris »

## 3. Shooting Illustrated (NRA) — « Rifle Warning Signs: When You Need to Stop Shooting »

- **Auteur :** Steve Adelmann, 16 octobre 2019
- **URL :** https://www.shootingillustrated.com/content/rifle-warning-signs-when-you-need-to-stop-shooting/

> « A jammed up firing-pin bore can stop pin movement and possibly fix the firing pin in the forward
> position. That may lead to a "runaway" machine gun, firing every time the bolt slams forward on a
> fresh round or worse, firing out of battery. »

> « Firearm-safety courses include warnings about "squib" loads that do not fully push a projectile
> out of the barrel. Hopefully, we notice this through a muted report and reduced recoil (referred
> to as "Pop-No-Kick") before attempting to fire another round. But, subsonic rifle ammo renders the
> differences between normal cartridges and squibs much less obvious. This is doubly true when sound
> suppressors and/or hearing protection are used. A good rule of thumb when using lower-power
> ammunition is that if you do not actually see or hear each round impact downrange, stop what you
> are doing, clear the rifle and check to ensure the bore is not obstructed before firing another
> round. »

> « Checking your brass will reveal piercing, but sometimes you get an earlier warning from a blast
> of gas in your face »

## 4. Wikipédia (anglais) — « Slamfire »

- **URL :** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slamfire

> « As rust, dirt, fouling, or inappropriately viscous lubricants accumulate in the firing pin
> channel, both free-floating and spring-loaded firing pins may be held in a forward position
> protruding from the bolt face. »

> « A single defective cartridge may cause a single slamfire, but a firing pin stuck in a forward
> position or a magazine loaded with defective ammunition may cause a round to fire every time the
> bolt closes until the magazine is empty. »

## 5. Outdoor Life — « Does Muzzle Tape Actually Affect Rifle Accuracy? »

- **Auteur :** Tyler Freel (chasseur en Alaska), 19 avril 2022
- **URL :** https://www.outdoorlife.com/guns/does-muzzle-tape-actually-affect-rifle-accuracy/

> « Electrical tape is the best thing I've found to keep moisture and debris out of my rifle muzzle.
> [...] I even keep extra tape rolled around the barrel just in front of the stock. Once I shoot
> through the first tape, I'll eventually retape it with the extra roll. »

> « The distinction though, is that when you put tape over your rifle muzzle, you are not placing
> anything inside the bore. »

> « In fact, the bullet never even contacts the tape. The air pressure created by the tightly sealed
> bullet traveling down the bore blows the tape away from the muzzle long before the bullet ever
> gets there. »

> « Average Group Center Shift: 0.273 inches »

## 6. Le renflement vu dans l'âme — ce qui n'est pas capté

La description « un anneau sombre dans l'âme regardée contre la lumière ; une bosse sous les doigts »
ne se trouve que sur des forums de tireurs (Rimfire Central, Muzzleloading Forum, Gunboards), vus en
résultats de recherche le 2026-09-24 et **non enregistrés**. Elle porte `[À VÉRIFIER]` dans la note 1
du chapitre. « Un canon renflé ne s'arrange pas en tirant » vient de NRA Women (voir
`35b-acier-dans-les-vieux-chokes.md` : « a problem in the barrel is certainly not going to get
better »).

## Ce que les sources ne disent pas

- **Le sens dans lequel on repousse une balle coincée, et le geste** : aucune source consultée ne le
  décrit (la source 2 dit seulement « using a cleaning rod remove any obstruction »). `[À VÉRIFIER]`
  à l'étape 37.
- **La baguette marquée à la longueur du canon** (étape 38) : proposée par le vérificateur technique ;
  c'est une mesure, la source 1 dit déjà « running a cleaning rod the length of the bore ».
- Le ruban n'a été essayé que sur des carabines (source 5). Le chapitre ne le propose pas pour le
  fusil.
