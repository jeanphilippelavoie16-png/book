## Extraits

Pages 3-4, l'article cite Stefansson (1944, p. 234) :

> « If you are transferred suddenly from a diet normal in fat to one consisting wholly of
> rabbit you eat bigger and bigger meals for the first few days until at the end of about a
> week you are eating in pounds three or four times as much as you were at the beginning of
> the week. By that time you are showing both signs of starvation and of protein poisoning.
> You eat numerous meals; you feel hungry at the end of each; you are in discomfort through
> distention of the stomach with much food and you begin to feel a vague restlessness.
> Diarrhoea will start in from a week to 10 days and will not be relieved unless you secure
> fat. Death will result after several weeks. »

Page 5, parmi les exemples ethnographiques que l'article ajoute :

> « […] and were continually craving for fat meat. » (Marcy 1863 : 16)

## Limites

- C'est un **récit d'explorateur** rapporté par une revue d'anthropologie, pas une description
  clinique. La seule littérature médicale consultée (Bilsborough et Mann) ne nomme que la
  nausée, la diarrhée et la mort.
- « Death will result after several weeks » n'est **pas** le « 2 à 3 semaines » du tableau de
  `CLAUDE.md` ; la note n'avance aucun délai de mortalité.
