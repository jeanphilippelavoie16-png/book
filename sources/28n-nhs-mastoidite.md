## Extraits intégraux

> "Mastoiditis is a rare but serious infection that affects the mastoid bone behind the ear. It can be more common in children and should be diagnosed and treated quickly."

> "pain, soreness or tenderness behind the ear ; redness behind the ear ; swelling behind the ear, which can cause it to stick out ; tiredness and irritability ; hearing loss in the affected ear"

> "Mastoiditis can lead to serious problems such as permanent hearing loss or meningitis if it's not treated quickly."

> "The main treatment is antibiotics. These are given directly into a vein through a drip (intravenously)."
