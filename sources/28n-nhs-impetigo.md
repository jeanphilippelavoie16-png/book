## Extraits intégraux

> "Impetigo is a skin infection that's very contagious but not usually serious. Anyone can get it, but it's more common in young children."
> "Impetigo starts with red sores or blisters [...] The sores or blisters quickly burst and often leave crusty, golden-brown patches."
> "look a bit like cornflakes stuck to your skin ; get bigger and spread to other parts of your body ; be itchy and are sometimes painful"
> "The sores and blisters of impetigo commonly affect the face (around the nose and mouth) and hands."

> "If you do not get treatment, it can take between 7 and 21 days for impetigo to stop being contagious – this is usually when all the patches heal, dry out and crust over."

> "wash affected areas with soap and water and dry them thoroughly" ; "do not touch or scratch sores, blisters or crusty patches – this also helps stop scarring" ; "do not share flannels, towels, sheets or pillowcases" ; "do not prepare food for other people if you have impetigo"

> "Impetigo usually infects skin that's already damaged. You can get it if you have a minor injury like a cut, scratch or insect bite"
