## Définitions (p. 3) — extraits intégraux

> « Track patterns described as "direct register" indicate that the hind foot usually lands
> directly on top of the front track while an "overstep" indicates that the hind foot usually lands
> ahead of the front foot on the same side. The stride is the distance measured from the front of
> one foot to the front of the same foot as the animal travels. The trail width is the distance
> measured between the outermost tracks. »

> « The negative space in a track usually appears as an X shape for red fox and H shape for
> coyote. » (p. 4)

> « A telltale sign that a 'wolf' track is instead a large breed domestic dog is the presence of
> nearby human tracks. » (p. 5)

> « The overall shape of felid tracks is round and their palm pad makes up at least 50% of the
> overall track size. These two features distinguish cat tracks from dogs. » (p. 12)
