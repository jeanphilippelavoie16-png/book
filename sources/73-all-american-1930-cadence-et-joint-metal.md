## Extraits intégraux

### Cadence de la soupape à poids

> « Vent the Canner: Turn on your burner and allow the canner to vent a steady stream of steam
> for 10 minutes before placing the regulator weight on the vent pipe. Bring to Pressure: Bring
> the canner up to pressure, adjusting the heat as needed to maintain 1 to 4 jiggles per
> minute. »

> « Why does my #68 pressure regulator weight constantly release? Most likely your heat setting
> is too high. Lower your heat setting so the weight jiggles approximately one to four times per
> minute. »

> « The All American 1930 gauge is for reference only (with the regulator weight being the
> primary indicator of correct pressure) and does not require external testing. »

### Le joint métal sur métal (l'appareil sans joint de caoutchouc)

> « Lubricate Seal: Apply a light coat of olive oil to the metal-to-metal seal area (the top edge
> of the bottom half of the canner). »

> « 1. Apply a thin film of lubricant (extra virgin olive oil or Vaseline) to the metal-to-metal
> seal (to the edge inside the cooker where the inside wall begins to bevel out). You only need
> enough lubricant to wet the edge, but not enough to see it. 2. Be sure that you are applying
> more lubricants as needed between batches. 3. Tighten two opposite wing nuts at a time; making
> sure that there is an even gap all the way around. »

> « 4. You may also clean the metal-to-metal seal. After wiping the old lubricant off with a soft
> cloth, use .0000 steel wool, a red 3M Scotch Brite Pad, or an SOS Pad to buff in a circular
> motion where the cover and bottom meet. Wipe down the sealing surface again using a soft cloth
> and be sure to lubricate the metal-to-metal seal after cleaning. »

> « Many new units require a break-in period before sealing properly. It may take several uses
> (sometimes up to 10) for the aluminum to naturally expand and conform to the lid, especially
> with our metal-to-metal seal design. »
