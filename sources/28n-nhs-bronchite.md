## Extraits intégraux

> "Bronchitis is inflammation of the airways in the lungs that is usually caused by an infection. It often gets better without treatment in around 3 weeks."

Symptômes : "a cough – you may cough up mucus ; chest pain when coughing ; shortness of breath ; a sore throat ; a runny nose ; a high temperature"

> "try adding honey to a warm drink to help soothe your throat (do not give honey to babies under 1)"

Signes immédiats : "you are struggling to breathe – you are choking, gasping and unable to speak ; your lips or skin are turning very pale, blue or grey – on brown or black skin this may be easier to see on the palms of the hands, on the lips, tongue or gums, under the nails or around the eyes ; you suddenly feel confused – for example, you do not know where you are"

Signes urgents : "you've had a cough for more than 3 weeks and it is not getting better ; you cough up blood or blood-stained mucus ; you have chest pain that comes and goes, or when breathing or coughing"
