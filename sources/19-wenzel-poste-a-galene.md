## Extraits intégraux

### Section « Simple Crystal Radio »

> « Some experimenters are tempted to omit the 82k resistor which discharges the capacitor on the
> theory that it wastes precious signal power. With a typical germanium diode, this little
> "improvement" may work somewhat but only because the diode has significant leakage and the
> performance will not be predictable. A dynamic earphone may be DC coupled eliminating the need
> for the resistor. »

> « Not all crystal earphones are sensitive and the experimenter should test a few to get a "good"
> one. High impedance dynamic earphones are a bit more reliable and can give excellent results. »

> « Try an old telephone receiver or a modern portable tape player headset (some are high-Z and
> fairly sensitive). »
