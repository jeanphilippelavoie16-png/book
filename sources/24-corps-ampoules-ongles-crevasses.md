## 1. Ampoules

### Lipman et coll. 2016, *Clin J Sport Med* — ruban de papier (résumé PubMed 27070112)

> « Paper tape was applied to a randomly selected foot before the race, either to participants'
> blister-prone areas [...] Paper tape reduced blisters by 40% (P < 0.01, 95% confidence interval,
> 28-52) with a number needed to treat of 1.31. [...] most common locations on the toes (n = 58,
> 50%) and heel (n = 27, 23%), with 94 (80%) blisters occurring by the end of stage 2. »

### Worthing et coll. 2017, *Wilderness Environ Med* — revue systématique (PubMed 28602272)

> « there is a paucity of high-quality quality evidence in support of socks, antiperspirants, or
> barriers for the prevention of friction blisters. Moderate confidence in effect estimate
> suggests that paper tape may be an effective form of barrier prevention. »

### Kirkham et coll. 2014, *Skin Res Technol* — peau humide (PubMed 24645912)

> « In controlled experimental conditions increased skin surface hydration increases the rate of
> temperature change of the skin in response to load application and consequently increases the
> risk of blister creation. »

(Pour le percement, le coussin en beigne et le toit laissé en place : fiche
`24-corps-nhs-aad-peau-mains-pieds.md`, section AAD.)

## 2. Ongle incarné

### *Indian J Dermatol* 2024 — bande glissée sous le coin de l'ongle (PMC11623420)

- **Copie :** `sources/24-corps-pmc11623420-ongle-incarne-bande.xml`

> « Proper trimming technique allows the corner of the nail to project beyond the edge of the
> skin. The toenails should be cut straight across, instead of rounded at the edges. Cutting the
> nail too short allows bulging of soft tissue leading to inflammatory reaction and pressure
> necrosis. »
> « Common conservative techniques for treatment in early stages include warm water soaks, topical
> or oral antibiotics, elevation of the corner of the nail with a cotton wick, band-aid method,
> nail wiring, gutter splint technique. »
> « Steri-strip TM is inserted between the disto-lateral edge of the nail plate and nail fold and
> secured with an elastic bandage. The dressing is kept till the nail edge grows over the nail
> fold and stops penetrating the skin. »
> « After 6 months, 46 out of 50 patients were satisfied with the treatment and showed no
> recurrence. »

### *Ann Dermatol* 2026 (PMC12868718) et *J Cosmet Dermatol* 2025 (PMC12406290)

- **Copies :** `sources/24-corps-pmc12868718-ongle-incarne-mesures.xml` ;
  `sources/24-corps-pmc12406290-ongle-incarne-enfant.xml`

> « Taping, dental floss placement, protective tubes, and gutter splints are simple and effective
> conservative measures appropriate for mild cases of ingrowing nails. However, in more advanced
> cases characterized by chronic inflammation and granulation tissue formation, these approaches
> are often insufficient »
> « taping of periungual folds—pulling the nail folds downwards using kinesiotaping tapes,
> threading the nail plate—lifting the distal parts of the nail plate using dental floss »

## 3. Crevasses (fissures) des doigts et des talons

### MASCC 2011, *Support Care Cancer* — guide de pratique (PMC3128700)

- **Copie :** `sources/24-corps-pmc3128700-fissures-cyanoacrylate-mascc.xml`

> « Thick moisturizers or zinc oxide creams can be applied. [...] Liquid glues like cyanoacrylate
> preparations (Superglue® or Liquid Bandaid®) can be used to seal the cracks and keep them from
> worsening or becoming infected. Sealing the cracks with these products can also help relieve
> pain and allow for healing. »
> « Wear protective footwear and avoid friction with fingertips, toes, and heals »

### *An Bras Dermatol* 2020 — recommandations d'experts (PMC7175407)

- **Copie :** `sources/24-corps-pmc7175407-fissures-cyanoacrylate-2020.xml`

> « For fissuring, protective coverings such as hydrocolloid, biological glue or even cyanoacrylate
> glue to relieve pain and promote healing, barrier creams (petroleum jelly, zinc oxide cream) and
> thick emollients might be used. »

*Limite :* les deux guides visent les fissures causées par des médicaments contre le cancer. Le
mécanisme (une fente de la couche cornée qui s'ouvre à chaque flexion) est le même que la
crevasse d'hiver ; la transposition est notée au suivi.

### *Medicina* 2022 — ingrédients des hydratants (PMC9315586)

- **Copie :** `sources/24-corps-pmc9315586-hydratants-occlusifs.xml`

> « Fatty acids are esters (R-COOH), the main component of animal oils and fats »

### DermNet — Hand dermatitis

- **URL :** https://dermnetnz.org/topics/hand-dermatitis
- **Copie :** `sources/24-corps-dermnet-dermatite-des-mains.html`

> « Irritants include water, detergents, solvents, acids, alkalis, cold, heat, friction, and
> desiccating dusts. These can damage the outer stratum corneum, removing lipids and disturbing
> the skin's barrier function. »
> « Apply thick emollients before work/school and reapply after washing or when the skin dries
> out (this can be 10–20 times in a day). »
> « Cotton gloves may be useful for irritating dry work when a waterproof outer glove is not
> required. »
> « Sweating under the gloves aggravates dermatitis. »
