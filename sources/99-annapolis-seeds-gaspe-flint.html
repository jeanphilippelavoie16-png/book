Gaspé Flint Corn

Remarkably early dwarf flint corn. Grown for centuries by the Mi'kmaq of the Gaspé Peninsula. The plants only reach a height of 2 to 3 feet, with cobs about 4" long. From late May sowing we had dry cobs ready to harvest on July 20, a mere 60-70 days. Plants can be spaced close together because of their small size. They aren't as productive as full sized varieties, but their short season allows them to grow in climates other corns can't. 40 seeds

Growing Info
Sowing Direct sow after risk of frost, or start indoors 4 weeks early
Spacing 6-8" between plants, 9-18" between rows.
Days to maturity 70-80
Soil Average to fertile, well draining
Light Full sun
