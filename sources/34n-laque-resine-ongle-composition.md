## Sipponen P., Sipponen A., Lohi J., Soini M., Tapanainen R., Jokinen J. J., « Natural coniferous resin lacquer in treatment of toenail onychomycosis: an observational study », *Mycoses*, 2013

- **URL :** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3666097/ (texte intégral, PMC)
- **Copie brute :** `34n-sipponen-2013-laque-resine-ongle-pmc3666097.html`

Extraits intégraux :

> « The salves and lacquers composed of purified resin from spruce are antifungal, particularly
> against dermatophytes in agar diffusion tests. The antifungal activity is maximal when the resin
> content (w/w) in salve or lacquer is 30% or more. »

> « The lacquer (Abicin Resin Lacquer 30%; Repolar Oy, Espoo, Finland) was constructed and
> manufactured from resin of the natural coniferous Norway spruce (Picea abies) originating from the
> Finnish Lapland. The resin content (w/w) of the lacquer was 30%. The other ingredients were
> monopropylene glycol, glycerol and alcohol. The lacquer was given to patients for self-medication
> in 10 ml bottles equipped with an applicator brush. The patients were asked to apply the solution
> daily on the affected nails. The solution dries in 30 min and forms a soft spotless cover on the
> nail. Before application, the patients were advised to wash and bathe the nail in warm water, and
> then to file and cut the nail. »

> « No trials or scientific reports on the clinical efficacy of coniferous resin in the treatment of
> onychomycosis or fungal skin diseases are available in spite of the fact that home-made coniferous
> resin salves have been used for centuries as a traditional remedy for various skin diseases in
> Northern Europe. »

## Ce que le chapitre en tire

- La laque des essais n'est pas un onguent durci : c'est de la résine **dissoute dans de l'alcool**
  (avec propylène glycol et glycérine), **30 % du poids**, posée au pinceau, qui sèche en
  **30 minutes** en une couche **souple**.
- Au laboratoire, la résine freine le plus les dermatophytes **à partir de 30 % du poids**, en
  onguent comme en laque : c'est le seul chiffre transposable au chalet.
- Avant chaque couche : ongle trempé à l'eau chaude, coupé et limé.
- Aucune recette de laque faite à la maison n'a été essayée.
