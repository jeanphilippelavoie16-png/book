Book Bindings

All Lulu products are professionally bound using the most popular, modern binding options. Every book or calendar is crafted to the highest industry standards, with durable binding methods designed for quality, longevity, and a professional finish.

Interior Color Options

Print your book using black & white ink or full color. Both options come in Standard and Premium options, ensuring you have the ink you need to print your book exactly the way you want. Lulu always uses Premium Color for your book’s cover.

Standard Black & White

Standard black & white printing for novels, memoirs, and other text-heavy books.

Premium Black & White

Heavy ink coverage great for books with black & white images, graphs, or other graphics.

Standard Color

For books that are predominantly text but have a few color images.

Premium Color

The best color printing on every page with rich colors and heavy ink coverage.

Cover Finish

Choose from matte or glossy for your paperback or casewrap cover finish.

Glossy

Stiff cover stock with a glossy coating finish. Glossy covers withstand wear and tear well while emphasizing the cover imagery. Glossy finish is commonly used for gifts, textbooks, and art books, but is appropriate for a variety of projects.

Matte

A matte finish gives your cover a more subdued, natural look. Used most often with novels and notebooks, matte finish withstands scratches and wear better than any other cover finish.

FAQs

Why Should I Create & Have My Book Printed with Lulu?

With over 20 years of experience, Lulu combines professional-quality print-on-demand with global fulfillment. You’ll save time, reduce shipping costs, and access consistently high-quality materials with no need for inventory.

How Long Does It Take to Print & Ship My Book?

Lulu’s global printing network requires a production time of 3-5 business days, but your shopping cart will display the estimated production time specific to your order.

Business days are Monday - Friday, and the first printing day is the day after the order is placed. For example, an order placed on a Monday will be sent to the printer by Tuesday and is expected to print between Thursday (day 3) and the following Monday (day 5).
