## Extraits (mot pour mot)

> « In healthy, nonalcoholic people, moderate alcohol consumption initially may improve sleep by
> decreasing the length of time needed to fall asleep. Increased alcohol consumption, however,
> can disrupt sleep by disturbing the critical second half of the sleep period and by altering
> the proportions of important sleep stages. »

> « Compared with nonalcoholics, alcoholics experience a greater number of sleep problems, such
> as difficulty falling asleep and a decrease in total sleep time […] These problems occur not
> only during periods of heavy drinking, but also during alcohol withdrawal and following short
> or prolonged periods of abstinence. »

## Ce qu'on en tire pour le chapitre 38

Le verre pour dormir : il fait tomber plus vite, puis il brise **la deuxième moitié de la
nuit**. §5-G et §5-J.

## Ce que la page ne dit pas

Aucune dose, aucune heure. Le chapitre n'en donne pas.
