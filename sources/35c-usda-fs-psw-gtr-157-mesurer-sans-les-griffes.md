## Extraits intégraux — PSW-GTR-157

### La définition des mesures (section « Footprints »)

> « Prints may be measured at two levels: simple and complete. Simple measurements include width,
> length, and claw, metacarpal, and total lengths (fig. 2). Measure lengths parallel to the long
> axis of the foot; measure widths perpendicular to the long axis. **Length includes toe and
> interdigital pads but excludes the metacarpal pad on front feet.** Metacarpal length includes
> toe, interdigital, and metacarpal pads. **Total length is from the anterior tip of the claws to
> the posterior edge of the metacarpal pad.** Width is measured as the widest part of the track.
> Note whether the widest part of the track occurs at the interdigital pad or the toe pads. »

Figure 2 : « Morphology of the left front footprint of a wolverine and measurements commonly
recorded from carnivore tracks » — la « Claw Length » et la « Total Length » y sont des mesures
distinctes de la « Length ».

### L'avertissement sur les chiffres publiés (section « Mustelids »)

> « There are few published measurements of tracks and gait patterns for mustelids. Measurements
> given here summarize those in the literature and those of the authors. **It is often difficult to
> determine whether measurements in the literature include claws and metacarpal pads; those given
> here do not.** »

### La profondeur

> « Track size is influenced by the depth that the foot sinks into the surface; feet leave larger
> footprints in soft substrates than in hard ones. [...] A track that sinks into the surface may be
> several millimeters bigger than one on a hard surface. »

### Pour la photo

> « To make a photographic record of the print, place a ruler next to it, but do not cover
> important features such as claws or hair drag-marks. »

## Extrait intégral — guide de l'Alaska (source des fourchettes du chapitre)

> « How should tracks be measured? 1. Length: measure the vertical length of an individual track.
> 2. Width: measure the widest part of the individual track perpendicular to the length. 3. Stride:
> measure the track pattern from the heel of the right foot to the heel of the right foot the next
> time it lands. 4. Straddle: measure the trail width across a group of tracks. »

Le guide ne dit pas si ses fourchettes comptent les griffes ; il dit avoir adapté ses allures de
Mark Elbroch, *Mammal Tracks and Sign*, livre non consulté.

## Lecture

- La **méthode** officielle est claire : la **longueur** d'une empreinte se prend **sans les
  griffes** ; la griffe se mesure à part (« claw length ») ou dans une « longueur totale » qui est
  une autre mesure.
- Pour les **fourchettes** de l'Alaska reprises dans le chapitre, rien ne dit si les griffes y
  sont : le PSW-GTR-157 prévient justement que la littérature le dit rarement.
