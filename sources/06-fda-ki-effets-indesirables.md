## Passages retenus (cités tels quels)

> « An allergic reaction can have more serious symptoms. These include fever and joint
> pains; swelling of parts of the body (face, lips, tongue, throat, hands, or feet);
> trouble breathing, speaking, or swallowing; wheezing or shortness of breath. »

Iodisme : « metallic taste, burning mouth and throat, sore teeth and gums, symptoms of a
head cold », « upset stomach and diarrhea ». Autres : « skin rashes », « swelling of the
salivary glands ».

> « Individuals with multinodular goiter, Graves' disease, and autoimmune thyroiditis
> should be treated with caution -- especially if dosing extends beyond a few days. »
