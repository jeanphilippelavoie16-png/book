## 1. *Guidelines on Basic Newborn Resuscitation* (2012)

- **URL :** https://iris.who.int/handle/10665/75157 (fichier :
  https://iris.who.int/server/api/core/bitstreams/e348f5c5-6841-409f-8b13-cf58e4da03e0/content)
- **Copie :** `29-femmes-oms-reanimation-nouveau-ne-2012.pdf`
- Recommandation 1 : cordon « not be clamped earlier than one minute after birth » ;
  « When newly-born term or preterm babies require positive-pressure ventilation, the cord should
  be clamped and cut to allow effective ventilation to be performed. » Note : « normally take around
  3 minutes ».
- Recommandation 2 : « Newly-born babies who do not breathe spontaneously after thorough drying
  should be stimulated by rubbing the back **2-3 times** before clamping the cord and initiating
  positive-pressure ventilation. »
- Recommandation 3 : liquide clair, bébé qui respire : pas d'aspiration ; bébé qui ne respire pas :
  aspirer « only if the mouth or nose is full of secretions ».
- Recommandation 6 : sans appareil, « a bulb syringe [...] is preferable to a mucous extractor with a
  trap in which the provider generates suction by aspiration. »
- Recommandation 7 : « positive-pressure ventilation should be initiated **within one minute after
  birth**. »
- Recommandation 8 : ventiler « with air ».
- Recommandation 11 : vérifier le cœur « after 60 seconds of ventilation with visible chest
  movements ».
- **Recommandation 12** : « In newly-born babies who do not start breathing within one minute after
  birth, **priority should be given to providing adequate ventilation rather than to chest
  compressions**. »
- **Recommandation 13** : « no detectable heart rate after **10 minutes** of effective ventilation,
  resuscitation should be stopped » ; « heart rate below 60/minute and no spontaneous breathing
  after **20 minutes** of resuscitation, resuscitation should be stopped. »
- Environ le quart des décès néonatals dans le monde sont causés par une hypoxie à la naissance.

## 2. *Essential Newborn Care 1 — Immediate Care and Helping Babies Breathe at Birth*, guide du
prestataire (mai 2024)

- **URL :** https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/mca-documents/nbh/enc-course/revised-resources/print-files/print-who-enc-1---provider-guide---may-2024.pdf
- **Copie :** `29-femmes-oms-soins-essentiels-nouveau-ne-2024.pdf`
- Cordon (p. 20) : « Wait 1 - 3 minutes to clamp or tie and cut the cord so the baby receives blood
  from the placenta. » « Place a clamp or tie about **2 fingerbreadths** from the baby's abdomen. Place
  another about **5 fingerbreadths** from the abdomen. Cut between the clamps or ties with a
  disinfected scissors or blade. Look for any bleeding or oozing of blood. If bleeding occurs, place a
  second clamp or tie between the first one and the baby's skin. Leave the cut end of the cord open
  to the air to dry. »
- Première heure (p. 22) : peau à peau « without interruption for at least one hour » ; « Monitor
  the baby's temperature and breathing every 15 minutes » ; « Feel the baby's skin (foot or forehead)
  to estimate temperature » ; « Look for rapid breathing (>60 breaths/minute) and chest indrawing ».
- Liste de ventilation (p. 58) : « **Ventilate at 40 breaths/minute (30-50 breaths/minute
  acceptable)** » ; améliorer la ventilation : replacer le masque, « Reposition head », « Clear the
  mouth and nose of secretions », « Open the mouth », souffler plus fort.
- Signes de danger (fiche de transfert) : « Fast breathing/chest indrawing ; Temperature <35.5 or
  >38 °C ; Not feeding ; No movement ; Convulsions ».

## 3. *Thermal Protection of the Newborn: a practical guide* (WHO/RHT/MSM/97.2, 1997)

- **URL :** https://iris.who.int/handle/10665/63986
- **Copie :** `29-femmes-oms-protection-thermique-nouveau-ne-1997.pdf`
- Température normale : « **36.5—37.5°C** ». Hypothermie : 36-36,5 °C légère ; 32-36 °C modérée ;
  moins de 32 °C grave.
- « **a naked baby exposed to an environmental temperature of 23°C (73.4°F) at birth, suffers the
  same heat loss as does a naked adult at 0°C** (32°F). »
- « most newborns at birth, if left wet and naked, cannot cope with an environmental temperature of
  less than 32°C. But if the baby is immediately dried, put skin-to-skin with the mother and covered,
  the delivery room temperature can be as low as **25—28°C**. »
- « when the temperature in the room is becoming too warm for adults in short sleeves it is the right
  one for the newborn. »
- Étape 1 : pièce « clean, warm (at least 25—28°C), and free from draughts » ; « it might be easier to
  warm a small area of a room rather than the whole room » ; « two absorbent towels [...] a cap, a
  sheet or blanket » ; « In cool weather a source of heat should be available to prewarm the clothes
  and towels. »
- Étape 2 : sécher tout de suite, tête comprise ; « covered with a second, dry towel (discard the
  first towel) and a cap put on its head. »
- Étape 3 : peau à peau ; étape 4 : allaiter « preferably within an hour » ; étape 5 : bain retardé.
- « the smaller and more premature the newborn is, the less it tolerates cold and heat. »
