## Extraits intégraux

> « Its hydrodynamics are primarily controlled by the St. Lawrence River runoff, tides and ice
> cover influencing the physico-chemical factors, such as temperature and salinity throughout the
> seasons »

> « During each campaign, between 6 and 37 stations were sampled in the coastal zone at depths
> between 3 and 35 m and < 1 km from the coast, between Longue-Rive and Pointe-des-Monts »

> « Mean salinity ranged between 23.67 ± 1.16 and 31.20 ± 0.24 and was higher in October compared
> to July (Table S5), except for 2021, when no significant difference was found between months »

> « Coastal waters were less salty in July than in October due to the freshet, especially in the
> sectors of Manicouagan and Forestville, where river runoffs of several tributaries bring more
> freshwater into the coastal zone in spring and early summer, as it has also been observed along
> the northern coast of the GSL where the Romaine River flows »

## Lecture

- Salinité moyenne de **23,7 à 31,2** (échelle pratique, sans unité ; à peu près des grammes de
  sels dissous par kilo d'eau). Moyennes de profils verticaux de 3 à 35 m : l'eau puisée à la grève,
  en surface, près d'une embouchure, peut être plus douce.
- Plus douce en **juillet** (crue des rivières) qu'en **octobre** ; la marée et la glace jouent
  aussi. Les sels dissous ne sont pas tous du chlorure de sodium.
