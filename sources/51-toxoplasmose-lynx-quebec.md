### Extraits intégraux

> « Cats are the definitive hosts for T. gondii. Feral cats are probably the main source of
> environmental contamination with infective eggs, but many species of wild cats can also act as
> definitive hosts. »

> « Domestic and wild cats are the only known definitive host for T. gondii, which means the
> protozoa only reproduce and create new eggs within infected cats. Eggs are released in the
> feces of infected cats for 1 to 2 weeks. The eggs develop and become capable of infecting a
> new host within 1 to 5 days. »

> « The tachyzoites enter neural and muscle tissue where they become bradyzoites (slowly
> multiplying life stage) which are encased within tissue cysts. »

> « Other carnivorous species and humans can become infected by consuming infective eggs or by
> eating meat of animals containing tissue cysts. »

> « Toxoplasmosis is known to occur in white-tailed deer, mule deer, elk, moose, pronghorn
> antelope, bison, bobcats, caribou, black bears, polar bears, mink, red foxes, raccoons,
> skunks, and many bird and rodent species. »

> « However, people with a weakened immune system can suffer severe illness, and when women
> become infected for the first time during pregnancy, serious birth defects can result. »

> « People can avoid becoming infected with T. gondii by taking certain precautions such as
> washing hands and materials (cutting boards, knives, etc.) thoroughly after working with raw
> meat, and cooking all meat thoroughly (a meat thermometer should read at least 135° F). »

(135 °F = 57 °C : seuil propre au toxoplasme. Le livre garde 74 °C pour tout le gibier, qui le
couvre.)

## Ce que le livre peut en tirer

- Le lynx n'est pas « transposé » du chat : il est **infecté au Québec** (44 % des lynx piégés
  en 2001, 14 % dans l'ouest du Québec en 2009-2010) et, comme tout félin, **c'est un hôte
  définitif** : il peut rejeter les œufs dans ses crottes, en plus de porter des kystes dans le
  muscle.
- Deux voies pour l'humain, les deux présentes au poste de dépouillement : **la viande** mal cuite
  (kystes) et **les œufs** des crottes portés à la bouche.
