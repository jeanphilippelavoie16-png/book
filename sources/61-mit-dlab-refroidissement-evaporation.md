## Extraits intégraux

### Le principe

> « The ECC and clay pot cooler devices in this study function on the principle of direct
> evaporative cooling, where heat is removed as water evaporates from the surface of the storage
> device. The evaporative cooling effect causes a decrease in temperature and an increase in the
> relative humidity inside the storage device, conditions that increase the shelf life of many
> vegetables. »

### Le gain, chiffré, et les conditions où il existe

> « In hot and dry climates (greater than 25 °C and less than 40% humidity) ECCs and clay pot
> coolers can be expected to provide a storage environment with humidity greater than 80% and
> temperature at least 8 °C lower than the maximum daily ambient temperature. »

> « Operating conditions: Specific conditions are required for evaporative cooling devices to
> operate effectively: Low relative humidity (less than 40 %); High temperature (daily maximum
> above 25 °C); Access to water, which must be added for evaporative cooling to work;
> Availability of shady, well-ventilated locations for ECCs and clay pot coolers »

> « If any of these key criteria cannot be met at the time when improved vegetable storage is
> needed, then ECCs or clay pot coolers may not provide sufficient benefits to justify their
> use. »

### Pas pour la viande

> « Non-electric evaporative cooling devices - such as ECCs and clay pot coolers - are not
> suitable for items that require sustained temperatures below 20 °C (medicine, meat, and dairy
> products) or foods that require a low humidity environment (onions, coffee, garlic, millet, and
> other grains). »

### L'ombre, le vent, l'humidité autour

> « Do not place them in direct sunlight, as the heat from the sunlight absorbed will cancel out
> the cooling from the increased evaporation rate of water. […] Exposure to wind and good
> ventilation increase the evaporation of water from the surface of the ECC or clay pot cooler.
> If used in a small room, increased humidity in that room will reduce the cooling effect. »

## Ce que le guide établit pour le chapitre 61

- Le gain **est chiffré** : **au moins 8 °C sous le maximum du jour**, mais seulement par temps
  **chaud et sec** (plus de 25 °C, moins de 40 % d'humidité relative), à l'ombre et au vent.
- Sous ces conditions, le guide ne promet rien ; dans l'air humide, le gain fond.
- **Pas pour la viande** : l'appareil ne tient pas une température soutenue sous 20 °C.
- Le chapitre avait raison sur le vent sec et la brume ; il lui manquait la limite pour la
  viande.

## Ce que le guide ne dit pas

Rien sur un climat froid et humide comme celui du bord du fleuve ; aucune mesure sous 25 °C.
