## Extraits intégraux

> "A skin abscess will often be round and feel painful and warm. The middle of the lump may feel soft when touched."
> "Pus may leak from the abscess and you may have a high temperature."

Non urgent : "a lump that is getting bigger ; a lump anywhere on your body that lasts more than 2 weeks ; a lump that is hard and does not move"
Urgent : "a lump that is painful, red or hot, or you feel hot, cold or shivery ; swelling or redness spreading away from the lump"

> "The abscess may need be drained to get rid of the pus. This usually involves a small operation to make a cut in the skin which will allow the pus to drain."
