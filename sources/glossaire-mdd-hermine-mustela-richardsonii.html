Mustela richardsonii

Bonaparte, 1838

American Ermine

1 / 4
Location:Canada, AB, CalgaryDate taken:1 January 1974Description:Side view in white winter coat. Formerly Mustela erminea, Ermine, Stoat, Short-tailed Weasel.

Distribution :

Tundra and forested regions of Nearctic, except most of Alaska and some Pacific coast islands. Most of Canada, northern conterminous USA south to C California, N New Mexico, N Iowa and Maryland.
