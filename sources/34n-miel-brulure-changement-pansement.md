# Pansement de miel sur brûlure — fréquence de changement

- **Chapitre qui s'en sert :** 34 — Soigner avec la forêt (§5-G, étape 28, `[À VÉRIFIER]` n° 4)

## 1. Zbuchea A., « Up-to-date use of honey for burns treatment », *Annals of Burns and Fire Disasters*, vol. 27, 2014 (revue)

- **URL :** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4158441/ (texte intégral, PMC)
- **Copie brute :** `34n-zbuchea-2014-miel-brulures-pmc4158441.html`

Extraits intégraux :

> « Honey is then spread on the wound before being covered with a dry sterile gauze dressing. The
> amount of honey used varied from a thin layer (applied 2-3 times per day), to a thick layer or,
> more often, pouring the honey over the wound. »

> « Dressing changes, mostly daily, varied from 2-3 times per day to once every 2-3 days,
> depending also on the appearance and evolution of the wounds (clean wounds with reduced exudate
> require less frequent dressing changes). Liquid honey can be used on large areas (be it naturally
> fluid or made so through vigorous stirring or by heating below 40°C). Crystallized honey can
> easily be made fluid through careful heating. »

> « Thus, dressing changes are painless with no risk of damage or tearing of newly formed tissue. »

## 2. Subrahmanyam M., « Topical Application of Honey for Burn Wound Treatment - an Overview », *Annals of Burns and Fire Disasters*, vol. 20, 2007

- **URL :** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3188068/ (texte intégral, PMC)
- **Copie brute :** `34n-subrahmanyam-2007-miel-brulures-pmc3188068.html`

Extrait intégral :

> « In minor burns, it is recommended to pour tap water immediately on burns, as this reduces the
> temperature. Afterwards, honey can be applied. Depending on the area, 15-30 ml of honey can be
> applied directly onto the burn wound or soaked in gauze before application. Occlusive or absorbent
> secondary dressings are applied to prevent honey from oozing out, and the frequency of dressing
> changes depends on how rapidly the honey is diluted by the exudate, which declines as treatment
> progresses. »

## Ce que le chapitre en tire

Le pansement de miel se change **le plus souvent chaque jour** ; plus souvent tant que la plaie coule
et délaye le miel, moins souvent (tous les 2 ou 3 jours) quand elle est propre et coule peu. Le
changement ne fait pas mal et n'arrache pas la peau neuve.
