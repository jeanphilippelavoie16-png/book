How many pages can my book have?

  • Updated July 09, 2026 18:05

Every Blurb book format has a minimum and maximum page count. Before you check the limits for your format, it helps to understand how pages are counted. 

How pages are counted

Pages in a Blurb book are counted the same way as any book you’d pick up off a shelf. Page 1 appears on the right-hand side of the first sheet of paper. Page 2 is on the reverse of the same sheet.

Pages 2 and 3 face each other and form the first page spread — two facing pages printed on the front and back of adjacent sheets.

Minimum and maximum page counts by format

Page count limits depend on your format and, for photo books, the paper type you choose. 

*5x5 Mini Photo Book has a minimum of 20 pages with a maximum of 80 pages. Available in Premium Lustre or Matte paper only.

**Magazine formats need a final page count in a multiple of 4 (for instance 28, 32, 36, 40, etc)
