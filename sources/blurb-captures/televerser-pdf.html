How to upload a PDF to Blurb through PDF to Book

  • Updated July 21, 2026 14:26

Use this article if you’ve created your PDF in a tool outside of the Blurb plug-in for Adobe InDesign. If you’re using Adobe InDesign, use the Blurb plug-in instead. See: Get started with the Blurb plug-in for Adobe InDesign

Before you upload

Check your PDF against Blurb’s specifications and review it in Acrobat before uploading. These steps reduce the risk of preflight errors and print defects.

  • Check specifications: PDF to Book specifications and checklist
  • Review your PDF: How to review your PDF before uploading to Blurb through PDF to Book

Upload your PDF

  1. Go to the PDF to Book PDF uploader 
  2. Under Your PDF Details, select how your files are structured:
    1. Cover & Pages in Separate Files — your cover is a separate PDF from your pages. This is the most common option. Two upload zones appear: one for the cover PDF (maximum 90MB) and one for the pages PDF (maximum 2GB).
    2. Cover & Pages in Single File — your cover and pages are in one PDF. One upload zone appears. The uploader will use page 1 as the front cover and the last page as the back cover.
  3. Drag your PDF file(s) into the upload zone(s), or click the zone to browse for files. The Next button activates once your files are attached.
  4. Click Next. 
  5. The uploader then detects your book size from the PDF dimensions and presents matching options under Your Book Size. Confirm the correct size is selected. If your size isn’t listed, your PDF dimensions may not match a supported Blurb format.
  6. Under Your Book Details, select your Paper type and Cover type from the dropdowns. Visual previews and specification details are shown for each option.
  7. Under Blurb Logo Page, choose whether to include the Blurb logo on the last page of your book. Select No Blurb Logo to remove it for an additional fee. 
  8. Under ISBN, select an ISBN option if required. Paperback and Hardcover books require an ISBN. If you have your own ISBN, select it here and you'll be prompted to apply it to your book post upload.
  9. Enter your Book Title and Author Name 
  10. Click Upload to Blurb.
  11. Once the upload completes, review your book in the uploader preview. Check trim areas, bleed, and gutter before placing your order. See: How to review the PDF uploader preview
  12. Once the preview looks correct, complete your order.
