## Extraits intégraux

> "Symptoms of pneumonia can start suddenly or gradually over a few days."
Symptômes : "a cough ; shortness of breath ; a high temperature ; chest pain ; an aching body ; feeling very tired ; loss of appetite ; making wheezing noises when you breathe – babies may also make grunting noises ; feeling confused – this is common in older people"

> "Depending on your symptoms, most people get better in 2 to 4 weeks."

> "do not take cough medicine – coughing helps your body get rid of the infection"

Signes immédiats : "you're struggling to breathe – you're choking, gasping and unable to speak ; you have pale, blue or blotchy skin, lips or tongue ; you're coughing up blood ; you suddenly feel confused – for example, you do not know where you are ; you cannot wake your baby or they feel floppy"
