There are no carports or garages; people who own a car or truck
park it in front of the house or just beside it.
     Because the houses must be built on deep foundations

neath freezing level), each house has a basement (a very common
way of building in Canada). The first level above the groLmd is
quite high (fig. 13). The basement is practically empty, except for
a wood-burning stove. The elderly use that space to prepare cari-

often claimed by teenagers who like to be able to play their loud
music without disturbing their parents (too much), a place where
they can have their own private entrance."
