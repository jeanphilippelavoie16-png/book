Populi gemmae

Authors: Prof. Dr. med. Peter Altmeyer, Prof. Dr. med. Martina Bacharach-Buhles

All authors of this article

Last updated on: 01.09.2025

Dieser Artikel auf Deutsch

Synonym(s)

Barking buds; Gemmae populi; Poplar buds; Turiones populi

Definition
This section has been translated automatically.

Populi gemma, also known as poplar buds (see also trembling poplar), is a drug with antiphlogistic, analgesic, antibacterial and spasmolytic effects. It can be used in herbal medicines for the external treatment of dermatitis solaris, chilblains and superficial skin lesions.

see also Populi cortex

Commission E Monograph: Treatment of dermatitis

ESCOP: not processed

HMPC: not processed

Empirical medicine: Inflammations of various origins

Ingredients
This section has been translated automatically.

Populi gemma contains salicylic alcohol derivatives (phenolic glycosides, including among others salicin, salicortin and populin), flavonoids and essential oil with alpha- and beta-caryophylls, cardines and (+)-bisabolol.

Effects
This section has been translated automatically.

Poplar buds promote wound healing and also have an antibacterial and anti-inflammatory effect.

Field of application/use
This section has been translated automatically.

Poplar buds are used for superficial skin injuries, sunburn, chilblains and hemorrhoids.

In veterinary medicine, poplar buds have long been used in horses to treat diseases of the musculoskeletal system due to their anti-inflammatory effect, which can be explained by the active ingredient salicylic acid they contain. Due to its antibacterial effect, it is also used for kidney and stomach problems.

Dosage
This section has been translated automatically.

Poplar buds are used as preparations for application on the skin. For this indication there is a monograph of Commission E. These should have a drug content of 20 to 30 %.

Undesirable effects
This section has been translated automatically.

In rare cases allergic skin reactions may occur.

Contraindication
This section has been translated automatically.

In case of a known hypersensitivity to salicates, poplar buds, propulis or Peru balsam, it should not be taken.

Interactions
This section has been translated automatically.

There are no known interactions with other drugs.

Trade names
This section has been translated automatically.

Combination preparations: Trembling poplar bark/leaves+goldrutweed+ash bark

Phytodolor® Tincture

Note(s)
This section has been translated automatically.

Poplar buds are mainly used in veterinary medicine as anti-inflammatory ointment. The drug is used very rarely in human medicine. Hamamelis products are apparently more effective (comparative studies are missing).
