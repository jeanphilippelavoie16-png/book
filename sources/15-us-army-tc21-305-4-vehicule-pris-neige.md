## Extraits (TC 21-305-4, verbatim tel que rendu)

> « Shovel clear path ahead of each wheel. Put boards, brush, or similar material in cleared
> paths to get better traction. »

> « If additional power is needed to extract vehicle when mired in snow, place transmission in
> "1" (first) and transfer case in "L" (low range). After vehicle is extracted from mired
> condition, immediately return transfer case to "H/L" (high lock range) position. »

> « If vehicle remains stuck, use wrecker or another vehicle equipped with winch to winch or tow
> stuck vehicle. If another vehicle is not available, use self recovery winch. »

## Extrait (FM 9-207, ch. 3, résumé de la recherche)

- Avant de stationner par grand froid, placer de petites branches ou un autre isolant sous les
  pneus pour qu'ils ne gèlent pas au sol.
