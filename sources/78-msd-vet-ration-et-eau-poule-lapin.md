> productivity, environmental temperature, and amount of physical activity. »

> intake of birds in a thermoneutral environment […] If a bird consumes a diet that has a higher

Sur l'eau :

> « Under thermoneutral conditions, a general guide is that birds will drink approximately twice
> as much water as the amount of feed. However, many factors influence water intake, including
> environmental temperature, relative humidity, salt and protein levels of the diet, birds'
> productivity (rate of growth or egg production), and the individual bird's ability to resorb
> water in the kidney. As a result, specific water requirements cannot be given for all
> circumstances. Water deprivation for > 12 hours has an adverse effect on growth of young poultry
> and egg production of layers; water deprivation for > 36 hours results in a marked increase in
> mortality of both young and mature poultry. »

## Extraits intégraux — lapin

> « A rabbit should always have an unlimited supply of hay, because they frequently need to nibble
> on it to keep peristalsis active. »

> « Ad lib timothy hay is usually recommended for the maintenance diet of adult rabbits. »

> « Adult pet rabbits not intended for breeding should be fed a high-fiber pelleted diet,
> restricted to ¼ cup/5 lb body wt/day to prevent obesity and maintain GI health. »

> « It has been shown in metabolic studies that a rabbit will consume water at a rate of
> approximately 120 mL/kg/day. This is about twice the amount usually calculated for an equally
> sized dog or cat. […] It has also been shown that a rabbit will drink significantly more water
> from an open bowl than a sipper bottle. »

## Ce que ça permet d'écrire

- **Pondeuse :** de l'ordre de **80 à 120 g de moulée par jour**, 110 g en moyenne, **par temps
  doux** ; la ration monte au froid (la source le dit sans chiffrer). La méthode du chapitre
  (peser sept jours) reste la seule façon d'avoir le chiffre d'hiver.
- **Eau de la poule :** environ **deux fois le poids de moulée** en eau par temps doux, donc de
  l'ordre de 0,2 L par poule et par jour. **Plus de 12 heures sans eau : la ponte baisse. Plus de
  36 heures : la mortalité monte nettement.**
- **Lapin :** foin **à volonté**, sans chiffre de grammes par jour ; eau **environ 120 ml par kg
  de poids vif et par jour** (un lapin de 4 kg : environ un demi-litre), et il boit davantage
  dans un bol ouvert qu'au biberon.
- **Non retenu :** le ¼ de tasse de granulés par 5 lb vise le lapin de compagnie qu'on empêche
  d'engraisser, pas un lapin de chair.
