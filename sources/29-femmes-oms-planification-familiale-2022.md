## Chapitre 18 — Méthodes d'observation de la fertilité (p. 301-316)

- Tableau p. 302 : Standard Days 5 / 12 ; TwoDay 4 / 14 ; ovulation 3 / 23 ; **symptothermique
  < 1 / 2**. « Reliable effectiveness rates are not available for the calendar rhythm method or the
  basal body temperature method. » « abstaining during fertile times is more effective than using
  another method during fertile times. »
- Température basale : « goes up slightly after the release of an egg » ; p. 310 : « takes her body
  temperature at the same time each morning before she gets out of bed and before she eats
  anything » ; « rise slightly—**0.2° to 0.5°C**—just after ovulation » ; relations protégées
  « until 3 days after the woman's temperature has risen ».
- **Standard Days Method** (p. 306) : « A woman can use the Standard Days Method if most of her
  menstrual cycles are **26 to 32 days** long. If she has **more than 2 longer or shorter cycles within
  a year**, the Standard Days Method will be less effective » ; « counting the first day of monthly
  bleeding as day 1 » ; « **Days 8 through 19** of every cycle are considered fertile days ». CycleBeads
  : « a color-coded string of beads » ; « On day 1—the first day of monthly bleeding—move the rubber
  ring to the red bead » ; « The next day move the ring to the next bead. Do this every day, even
  bleeding days. » « If monthly bleeding begins again before reaching the dark brown bead, her
  menstrual cycle is shorter than 26 days. » « If monthly bleeding does not begin before reaching
  the last brown bead, her menstrual cycle is longer than 32 days. »
- Calendrier : exige 6 mois de relevés (non retenu par le livre : paperasse, et « not effective »
  selon l'INSPQ).
- **TwoDay Method** (p. 310) : vérifier « every afternoon and/or evening, on fingers, underwear, or
  tissue paper or by sensation » ; « As soon as she notices any secretions of any type, color, or
  consistency, she considers herself fertile that day and the following day » ; « unprotected sex
  again after the woman has had **2 dry days** [...] in a row ». Difficile en cas d'infection
  vaginale.
- **Ovulation method** (p. 311-312) : jour du « pic » = dernier jour de glaire claire, glissante,
  élastique ; fertile jusqu'au 4e jour après le pic.
- **Symptothermique** (p. 312) : « The couple avoids unprotected sex between the first day of monthly
  bleeding and either the fourth day after peak cervical secretions or the third full day after the
  rise in temperature (BBT), whichever happens later. »
- Quand commencer (p. 309) : « No monthly bleeding: Delay symptoms-based methods until monthly
  bleeding returns » ; après l'accouchement, « once normal secretions have returned ».

## Chapitre 19 — Retrait (p. 317-318)

- « about 20 pregnancies per 100 women [...] over the first year » en usage courant ; « about 4 »
  bien utilisé. « Before sex he should urinate and wipe the tip of his penis to remove any semen
  remaining » s'il a éjaculé récemment. « Requires no supplies ».

## Chapitre 20 — MAMA (p. 319-320)

- Trois conditions : « The mother's monthly bleeding has not returned » ; « The baby is fully or
  nearly fully breastfed and is fed often, day and night » ; « The baby is less than 6 months old. »
- « As commonly used, about 2 pregnancies per 100 women using LAM in the first 6 months » ; « less
  than 1 » bien utilisée. « Frequent breastfeeding temporarily prevents the release of the natural
  hormones that cause ovulation. »

## Chapitre 14 — Condom masculin (p. 261-263)

- « Avoid using a condom past the expiration date. Do so only if a newer condom is not available. »
- Encadré p. 263 : « Clean water and saliva can be used for lubrication » ; « **Lubricants made with
  oil can damage latex**, so do not use the following with latex condoms: any oils (cooking, baby,
  coconut, mineral) or products made with oil, petroleum jelly, lotions, cold creams, cocoa butter,
  butter, margarine ».

## Durées des méthodes posées d'avance

- Stérilet au cuivre (p. 187-188 du PDF) : « Studies have found that the TCu-380A is effective for
  **12 years**. The TCu-380A is labeled for up to 10 years of use ».
- Stérilet au lévonorgestrel (p. 214 du PDF) : « Mirena and Kyleena are approved for up to **5 years**
  of use. Research shows that Mirena remains highly effective for 7 years. »
- Implants (p. 153 du PDF) : efficaces « for 3–5 years, depending on the type of implant ».
- Vasectomie (p. 263-264 du PDF) : condoms ou autre méthode « for **3 months** after the procedure ».

## Annexe B — Signes graves (p. 416)

- Grossesse extra-utérine : « Unusual abdominal pain or tenderness ; Abnormal vaginal bleeding or no
  monthly bleeding [...] ; Light-headedness or dizziness ; Fainting ».
- Thrombose veineuse profonde : « Persistent, severe pain in one leg, sometimes with swelling or red
  skin. »

## Contraception d'urgence (chapitre 3)

- « All ECPs should be taken as soon as possible for greatest effectiveness » ; l'ulipristal peut être
  plus efficace entre 72 et 120 heures.
