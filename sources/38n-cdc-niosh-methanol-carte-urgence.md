## Extraits (mot pour mot)

> « Most methanol poisonings occur as a result of drinking beverages contaminated with methanol or
> from drinking methanol-containing products. »

> « Adverse health effects from methanol poisoning may not become apparent until after an
> asymptomatic period of 1 to 72 hours. »

> « Initial adverse health effects due to methanol poisoning include drowsiness, a reduced level of
> consciousness (CNS depression), confusion, headache, dizziness, and the inability to coordinate
> muscle movement (ataxia). »

> « Early on after methanol exposure, there may be a relative absence of adverse health effects.
> This does not imply insignificant toxicity. Methanol toxicity worsens as the degree of metabolic
> acidosis increases, and thus, becomes more severe as the time between exposure and treatment
> increases. »

> « Ophthalmologic: visual disturbances, blurred vision, sensitivity to light (photophobia), visual
> hallucinations (misty vision, skin over the eyes, snowstorm, dancing spots, flashes), partial to
> total loss of vision »

> « The most common permanent adverse health effects following severe methanol poisoning are damage
> to or death of the nerve leading from the eye to the brain (optic neuropathy or atrophy),
> resulting in blindness »

Synonymes donnés par la page : « Methyl alcohol », « Wood alcohol », « Methyl hydrate ».
