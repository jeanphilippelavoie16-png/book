## 1. Gallardo A., Toloza A., Vassena C., Picollo M. I., Mougabure-Cueto G., « Comparative efficacy of commercial combs in removing head lice (*Pediculus humanus capitis*) », *Parasitology Research*, vol. 112, p. 1363, mars 2013

- **URL :** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00436-012-3208-z (résumé ; article complet payant)
- **Copie brute :** `34n-gallardo-2013-peignes-a-poux-ecartement.html`

Extrait intégral (résumé) :

> « The use of a fine comb for removing lice from the head of the human host is a relevant tool both
> in the diagnosis of infestations and as part of an integrated control strategy of head lice. The
> effectiveness of a fine comb depends, in part, on the design and material they are built. The aim
> of this study was to compare in vivo the efficacy of metal and plastic combs that are currently
> used in the removal of head lice and eggs worldwide. The space between comb teeth and the length
> was 0.23 and 13 mm in KSL plastic, 0.3 and 10.7 mm in NOPUCID plastic, 0.15 and 31 mm in KSL metal
> and 0.09 and 37 mm in ASSY metal. [...] The most effective plastic comb was KSL, removing a higher
> number of individuals of all stages. The most effective metal comb was ASSY, removing more insects
> of all stages (except adults). The comparative test between KSL plastic and ASSY metal showed that
> ASSY was the most effective in removing head lice and their eggs. »

## 2. University of California, Statewide IPM Program — Pest Notes « Head Lice »

- **URL :** https://ipm.ucanr.edu/home-and-landscape/head-lice/ (mise à jour 07/2020)
- **Copie brute :** `34n-uc-ipm-poux-de-tete.html`

Extraits intégraux :

> « Lice eggs are called nits. Nits are oval, 1/32 inch long (0.8 mm), and camouflaged (for
> light-colored hair) with the host's own hair pigment. »

> « Nits are most effectively removed by combing wet hair with a specially designed nit comb. The
> most effective nit comb is a metal one specially designed for removing nits. [...] Plastic combs
> may or may not be effective. »

> « If the infestation is not extensive, selectively cutting those hairs to which eggs are attached
> with small safety scissors is an option, but this is rarely practical. »

## Ce que le chapitre en tire

Une lente fait **0,8 mm** de long. Les peignes mesurés laissent **0,09 à 0,3 mm** entre deux dents ;
le plus serré (métal, 0,09 mm) enlève le plus de poux et d'œufs, et le plastique à 0,23-0,3 mm en
enlève moins. Un peigne taillé à la main n'approche pas du dixième de millimètre.
