## Extraits intégraux

Symptômes : "a high temperature ; feeling or being sick ; pain in your lower back or side ; diarrhoea ; aching muscles and flu-like symptoms ; pain, burning or stinging when you pee ; needing to pee more often and urgently than usual ; pee that's dark or cloudy"
Jeunes enfants : "wetting the bed or themselves ; being drowsy" ; "A child under 2 years may only have a high temperature."

Signes urgents : "have a very high temperature, or feel hot, cold or shivery ; have a very low temperature below 36C ; have pain in the lower tummy or in the back, just under the ribs ; have blood in your pee ; are feeling or being sick ; have not had a pee all day"

> "rest – it can take up to 2 weeks before you feel able to return to work"
> "drink lots of fluids, such as water or squash – take small sips if you feel sick"
> "do not take ibuprofen unless advised by a doctor – it may increase your risk of further kidney problems"
