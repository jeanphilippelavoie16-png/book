## Extraits intégraux utilisés

### Réfrigérateur

> The page specifies that fridge temperatures should be "between 0 °C and 4 °C" and that
> "food stored in it will stay cold for about four to six hours after a power outage."

> "during an electrical failure, it is important not to open the door of your appliance."

**À garder (si aucun signe de détérioration) :** fromages fermes, beurre, fruits et
légumes frais entiers, confitures, condiments commerciaux comme la moutarde et le ketchup.

**À jeter :** lait, fromages mous, contenants de yogourt ouverts, légumes cuits, toute
viande / volaille / poisson, desserts à base de crème, mayonnaise maison.

### Congélateur

> Full freezer: "48 hours in a properly functioning freezer that is filled to capacity"
> Half-filled freezer: "24 hours"

### Recongélation

> Partially thawed food "can be refrozen if the centre of the food is still hard"

> Thawed raw foods at "4 °C or below: can be cooked immediately for quick consumption or
> refrozen after cooking"

> Completely thawed perishables: "discard if exposed to temperatures above 4 °C"

### Mijoteuse

> "Food that has been interrupted from cooking in a slow cooker due to a prolonged power
> outage should not be eaten. It must be discarded."

### Règle générale de doute

> There is no risk to take with foods that show signs of deterioration or that have a
> suspicious appearance. In case of doubt, throw them out.

> Throw away or compost all other foods including meat, fish, eggs, milk, and prepared
> dishes, even if they appear normal.
