## Extraits intégraux

> "It's common and usually clears up on its own within 4 weeks."

> "pain, swelling and tenderness around your cheeks, eyes or forehead ; a reduced sense of smell ; green or yellow mucus from your nose ; a feeling of pressure in the ears"

> "Young children may also be irritable, have difficulty feeding and breathe through their mouth."

> "drinking plenty of fluids ; taking painkillers [...] ; cleaning your nose with a salt water solution"

> "Rarely, you may need antibiotics. This is not common as sinusitis is usually caused by a virus, not bacteria."
