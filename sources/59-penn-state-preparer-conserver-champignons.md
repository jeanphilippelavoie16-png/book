## Extraits intégraux

### Entreposage

> Always store mushrooms in the refrigerator, either in their original container or, as the
> *Guide* suggests, in a paper bag (for up to 3 days).

> When loose mushrooms are stored out of a paper bag, they can dry out; if they are stored in a
> plastic bag, moisture can collect on the mushrooms and spoil them.

(Le « Guide » cité est *Pennsylvania Produce: A Guide to Quality Produce Grown in PA*,
DiGuiseppe et Young, 2016.)

### Congélation (reprise du NCHFP)

> Choose mushrooms free from spots and decay. Thoroughly wash them in cold water and sort them by
> size. Trim the ends of the stems.

> Steam-blanch the mushrooms for 5 minutes for slices or 9 minutes for quarters or buttons. Cool
> the mushrooms quickly. Drain and package them, leaving ½-inch headspace. Seal and freeze them.

### Séchage (reprise du NCHFP)

> Scrub them well and remove and discard any woody stalks. Cut tender stalks into short sections.
> Slice them. Dry them in a dehydrator for 8–10 hours.

## Notes de captation

La page vise les champignons du commerce (champignon de Paris et apparentés). Le **3 jours** au
réfrigérateur est donc un plafond pour un champignon cultivé, cueilli et emballé proprement ; un
champignon sauvage rapporté du bois ne se garde pas plus longtemps. Le MDC dit « a few days »
pour les sauvages (`59-mdc-preparer-et-conserver-champignons.md`).
