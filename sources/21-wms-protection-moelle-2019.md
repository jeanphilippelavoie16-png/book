## Extraits intégraux (anglais, tels que publiés)

> « Patients requiring extrication should be encouraged to reduce movement of the neck, especially
> painful movement, and allowed to exit the situation under their own volition if alert and
> reliable. If injuries or other circumstances such as unconsciousness prevent controlled
> self-extrication, patients' cervical spines should be packaged to reduce passive motion and the
> airway adequately managed without a goal of absolute immobilization. There is no requisite role
> for commercially made or improvised rigid cervical collars in any out-of- hospital environment. »

> « Neutral alignment should be restored and maintained using nonrigid tools during extrication,
> unless such a maneuver is met with resistance, increased pain, or new or worsening neurologic
> deﬁcit. »

> « Other studies have shown that cervical collars can increase the risk of aspiration, directly
> compromise respiration, elevate intracranial pressure, cause pressure ulcerations, and conceal
> important physical findings [...] Patients with suspected injury should have their neck supported
> in a position of comfort. »

> « Backboards and other rigid carrying devices may be used for temporary patient movement if needed
> but should not be applied as a medical tool with an immobilization goal. »

> « If spinal cord protection is desired, appropriately trained personnel using either the NEXUS
> criteria or the Canadian C-spine rule can safely and effectively make decisions in the
> out-of-hospital setting regarding whether cervical spine motion should be reduced. »

## Lecture pour le livre

- Conscient, clair et fiable : il sort par ses propres moyens, en évitant les mouvements du cou qui
  font mal. On ne le tire pas pour l'aider.
- Inconscient, ou incapable de sortir seul : il sort couché, le cou calé pour limiter les mouvements
  passifs (rouleaux de vêtements, ch. 26, §9), voies respiratoires surveillées.
- Pas de collet rigide improvisé : il ne protège rien de démontré et gêne la respiration. Une surface
  ferme sert au transport.
- Les critères qui décident (NEXUS) : `21-mdcalc-criteres-nexus.md`.
