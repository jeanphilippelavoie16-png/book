## Extraits intégraux

> "A young child might have earache if they: rub or pull their ear ; do not react to some sounds ; have a temperature of 38C or above ; are irritable or restless ; are off their food ; keep losing their balance"

> "Most earaches get better on their own but get medical help if symptoms do not improve within 2 to 3 days."

> "place a warm flannel on the ear"

Tableau des causes : "Ear pain with toothache — Children teething, dental abscess" ; "Ear pain with pain when swallowing — Sore throat, tonsillitis, quinsy (a complication of tonsillitis)".

## Usage au ch. 28

§ 5-C et § 9 : la débarbouillette tiède sur l'oreille (seule mesure sans médicament sourcée pour la douleur d'oreille) ; l'oreille qui fait mal avec une dent (ch. 27) ou quand on avale (gorge, phlegmon).
