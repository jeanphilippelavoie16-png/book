## Extraits intégraux

Symptômes : "pain or a burning sensation when peeing (dysuria) ; needing to pee more often than usual ; needing to pee more often than usual during the night (nocturia) ; needing to pee suddenly or more urgently than usual ; pee that looks cloudy ; blood in your pee ; lower tummy pain or pain in your back, just under the ribs ; a high temperature, or feeling hot or cold, and shivery ; a very low temperature below 36C ; feeling tired or weak"

Enfants : "have a high temperature – your child is feeling hotter than usual if you touch their neck, back or tummy ; appear generally unwell [...] ; wet the bed or wet themselves ; be sick"

> "ask you to wait for 48 hours before getting antibiotics in case your symptoms go away on their own"

> "take paracetamol up to 4 times a day to reduce pain and a high temperature" ; "rest and drink enough fluids so you pass pale urine regularly during the day"

> "Some people who are not pregnant take cystitis sachets or cranberry drinks and products to prevent UTIs from happening, which may help. But there's no evidence they help ease symptoms or treat a UTI if the infection has already started."

> "Some of these could be symptoms of a kidney infection, which can be serious if it's not treated as it could cause sepsis."

Prévention : "wipe from front to back when you go to the toilet" ; "do not hold your pee in if you feel the urge to go" ; constipation chez l'enfant parmi les facteurs de risque.
