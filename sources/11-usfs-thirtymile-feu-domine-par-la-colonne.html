Lessons Learned
• Timber crown fires release enormous amounts of energy and do not always grow in a predictable fashion.
• Plume-dominated fires can create strong indrafts and downdrafts. The fire behavior that results can be severe and erratic. Indrafts and downdrafts may have contributed to the extreme, erratic fire behavior on the Thirtymile Fire.
