## Extraits intégraux

Guide P3010L-CO-CA, en cas d'alarme de CO :

> « Immediately move to fresh air – outdoors or by an open door/window. Do a head count to check
> that all persons are accounted for. Do not re-enter the premises nor move away from the open
> door/window until the emergency services responders have arrived, the premises have been aired
> out, and your alarm remains in its normal condition. »

> « This alarm has not been investigated for carbon monoxide detection below 70 PPM. »

> « Individuals with medical problems may consider using warning devices which provide audible and
> visual signals for carbon monoxide concentrations under 30 PPM. »

> « At 70 PPM, the unit must alarm within 60-240 minutes. At 150 PPM, the unit must alarm within
> 10-50 minutes. At 400 PPM, the unit must alarm within 4-15 minutes. »

Page 2 :

> « Per these requirements, standard CO sensors will typically not alarm at levels below 30 ppm. »

> « However, carbon monoxide alarms with ultra-sensitive detection will indicate low levels of CO
> below 30 ppm »

Page 3 :

> « Any time a digital unit displays a number with or without an alert, assume it senses carbon
> monoxide. »

## Lecture

- Le fabricant pose trois conditions pour rentrer : secours arrivés (sans objet au chalet), lieux
  **aérés**, avertisseur **revenu à son état normal**.
- Tout chiffre affiché = du CO. Un affichage à zéro ne prouve pas l'absence de CO : il dit que la
  concentration est sous ce que l'appareil sait lire.
