Vertical linocut print, hand-carved relief print with visible gouge marks, the ink goes to all four edges of the image with no border, no margin and no paper edge. Only three inks: very dark earth brown, cream paper and warm orange. A summer evening at a small log cabin in the boreal forest of the Quebec north shore. The upper half of the image is a calm dark brown sky, solid ink with a few long carved horizontal lines, empty for a title. A big orange setting sun low on the right, half behind low rounded forested hills that stay below the middle of the image. Black spruces and white birches in full summer leaf behind the cabin; the cabin has one glowing orange window and chimney smoke. To the left of the cabin a long open stack of firewood with round log ends under a narrow slanted roof. A split-rail fence. In the foreground a lush vegetable garden in rows of leafy potato plants and cabbages, and one chopping block stump with a single axe stuck in it. Folk art printmaking, soulful, handmade. The bottom edge is plain, unsigned. No text, no letters, no signature, no monogram, no people, no animals.
