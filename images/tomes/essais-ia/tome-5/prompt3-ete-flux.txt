Vertical linocut print, hand-carved relief print with visible gouge marks, the dark ink runs off all four edges of the image: full bleed, no border, no margin, no paper edge. Only three inks: very dark earth brown, cream paper and warm orange. A summer evening at a small log cabin in the boreal forest of the Quebec north shore. The upper half of the image is a calm dark brown sky, solid ink with a few long carved horizontal lines, empty for a title. A big orange setting sun low on the right, half behind low rounded forested hills that stay below the middle of the image. Black spruces and white birches behind the cabin; the cabin has one glowing orange window and chimney smoke. Beside the cabin a long open stack of firewood showing the round ends of the logs, under a narrow slanted roof. A split-rail fence. In the foreground a vegetable garden of dark soil with rows of pale cabbages and potato plants, a pale path to the cabin door, and one brown chopping block stump with a single axe stuck in it. Folk art printmaking, soulful, handmade. The bottom edge is plain dark soil, unsigned. No text, no letters, no signature, no monogram, no people, no animals.
