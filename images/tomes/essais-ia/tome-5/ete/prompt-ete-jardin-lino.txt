Linocut print of a midsummer evening at a log cabin in the boreal forest, hand-carved relief print with visible gouge marks and paper grain, three inks only: very dark earth brown, cream paper, warm orange. The upper half is a calm empty dark brown sky with a few carved horizontal lines. A big round orange setting sun low on the right, half behind low rounded hills, with carved rings. Spruce and white birch forest in full leaf behind a small log cabin with orange windows and a chimney. On the left a low log woodshed. In the foreground a vegetable garden in full summer growth on dark soil: rows of big leafy cabbages, potato plants and bean poles, every leaf carved in cream lines; a brown earth path between the rows to the cabin door; tall summer grass and wildflowers along the edges; a stump in the foreground.
