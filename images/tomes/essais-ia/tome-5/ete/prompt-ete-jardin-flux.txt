Full-bleed book cover illustration in the style of a hand-carved linocut: rough gouge marks and carving texture, paper grain, bold hand-cut shapes, Quebec folk art, unsigned, no border. Only three colours: very dark earth brown, cream, warm orange. A midsummer evening at a log cabin in the boreal forest, no snow anywhere. In the foreground a vegetable garden in full summer growth on dark soil: rows of big leafy cabbages and potato plants with every leaf carved in cream lines, a brown earth path to the cabin door, tall grass and wildflowers at the edges, a cut stump in the front. Soulful, quiet and warm. No text, no letters, no signature, no snow, no people, no animals.
