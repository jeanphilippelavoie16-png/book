Linocut print, hand-carved relief print with visible gouge marks and carving texture, printmaking paper grain, only three inks: very dark earth brown, off-white cream paper and warm orange. A summer evening at a small log cabin in the boreal forest of northern Quebec. The upper half of the image is a huge calm open sky in dark brown ink with a few long horizontal carved lines, empty and quiet, left free for a title. A large low orange setting sun sits on the horizon on the right, half hidden behind low rounded forested hills. Low distant rounded hills only near the middle of the image, never reaching into the sky. Middle ground: a dark forest of black spruces and a few white birch trees, a small log cabin with one glowing orange window and a chimney with a thin curl of smoke, a small woodshed with a lean-to roof full of a neatly stacked cord of split firewood. Foreground: a vegetable garden in long straight rows carved in perspective leading to the cabin on cream paper ground, a chopping block with an axe stuck in it and split logs beside it. Folk art printmaking, soulful, quiet and warm, slightly irregular hand-cut edges, full bleed. No mountain peaks, no text, no letters, no signature, no people, no animals.
