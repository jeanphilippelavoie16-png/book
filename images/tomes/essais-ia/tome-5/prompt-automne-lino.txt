Linocut print, hand-carved relief print with visible gouge marks and carving texture, printmaking paper grain, only three inks: very dark earth brown, off-white cream paper and warm orange. Late autumn afternoon at a small log cabin in the boreal forest of northern Quebec. The upper half of the image is a huge calm open sky in dark brown ink with a few long carved horizontal lines, empty and quiet, left free for a title. A low orange sun near the horizon on the left, just above low rounded forested hills. Low distant rounded hills only near the middle, never reaching into the sky. Middle ground: dark black spruces and white birch trees with a few orange leaves, a small log cabin with smoke rising from the chimney, a long high cord of split firewood stacked along the cabin wall under a small roof. Foreground: a harvested garden in carved rows with a few orange pumpkins on cream paper ground, a chopping block with an axe stuck in it, a pile of freshly split logs. Folk art printmaking, soulful, quiet and warm, slightly irregular hand-cut edges, full bleed. No mountain peaks, no text, no letters, no signature, no people, no animals.
