Vertical linocut print, hand-carved relief print with visible gouge marks, full bleed with no border and no margin, only three inks: very dark earth brown, off-white cream paper and warm orange. Summer evening at a small log cabin in the boreal forest of the Quebec north shore. The upper half is a huge calm sky printed in dark brown ink with a few long carved horizontal lines, empty for a title. A big orange setting sun low on the horizon on the right, half behind low rounded forested hills. Low rounded hills only below the middle of the image. A dark band of black spruces and white birches behind a small log cabin with a glowing orange window and chimney smoke. Beside the cabin a small woodshed with a slanted roof stacked full of split firewood. In the foreground a vegetable garden in long straight rows on cream paper leading to the cabin, and a chopping block with an axe stuck in it, split logs around it. Folk art printmaking, soulful, handmade. Unsigned. No text, no letters, no signature, no monogram, no people, no animals.
