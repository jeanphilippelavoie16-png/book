Vertical linocut print, hand-carved relief print with visible gouge marks, full bleed with no border and no margin, only three inks: very dark earth brown, off-white cream paper and warm orange. Autumn evening at a small log cabin in the boreal forest of the Quebec north shore. The upper half is a huge calm sky printed in dark brown ink with a few long carved horizontal lines, empty for a title. A big orange sun low on the horizon on the left, half behind low rounded forested hills. Low rounded hills only below the middle of the image. A dark band of black spruces and white birches with orange leaves behind a small log cabin with chimney smoke. Along the cabin wall a long high cord of split firewood under a small slanted roof. In the foreground a harvested vegetable garden in carved rows on cream paper with a few orange pumpkins, and a chopping block with an axe stuck in it, split logs around it. Folk art printmaking, soulful, handmade. Unsigned. No text, no letters, no signature, no monogram, no people, no animals.
