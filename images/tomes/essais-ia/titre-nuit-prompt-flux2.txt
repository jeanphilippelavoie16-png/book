Vertical linocut print, hand-carved relief print with visible gouge marks, only three inks: deep navy blue, off-white paper and warm orange. Winter night on the north shore of the St. Lawrence in Quebec. The upper half is a huge open starry sky with carved stars and a full moon low on the right, calm and empty for a title. Low rounded forested hills only at the horizon, below the middle of the image. A wide frozen river, a far shore of tiny black spruces. Lower third: deep snow with carved drift lines, tall slender black spruces heavy with snow framing both sides, a small log cabin with two glowing orange windows, smoke curling from the chimney, a woodpile, snowshoe tracks leading to the door. Folk art printmaking, soulful, handmade. No text, no letters, no signature, no people, no animals.
