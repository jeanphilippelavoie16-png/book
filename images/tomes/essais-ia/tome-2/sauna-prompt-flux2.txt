Vertical linocut print, hand-carved relief print with visible gouge marks and paper grain, printed with only three inks: a very dark oxblood maroon that is almost black (dark dried-wine colour), off-white paper, and small touches of warm orange. Winter night at the edge of a frozen lake in the boreal forest of northern Quebec. The upper half of the image is a huge calm night sky printed as one flat solid area of very dark oxblood maroon, almost black, with a few tiny carved white stars, completely empty for a title, no gradient, no sunset. Low rounded dark hills only at the horizon, below the middle of the image, with a thin orange rim of light on their crests. A wide frozen lake of white snow, a far shore of tiny dark spruces with one small distant cabin showing two orange windows. Lower third: a small low log smoke sauna on the near shore, its wooden door half open with orange firelight inside and a soft orange glow on the snow just in front of the door, thick white steam and smoke rising from the roof in carved swirls. White snow with carved drift lines and a few snowshoe tracks, tall slender dark spruces heavy with snow framing both sides. Folk art printmaking, soulful, handmade, full bleed to the edges. No text, no letters, no signature, no monogram, no border, no people, no animals, no mountains.
