Vertical linocut print, hand-carved relief print with visible gouge marks and paper grain, printed full bleed with only three inks: very dark oxblood maroon, off-white paper and warm orange. Late afternoon in summer at the edge of a calm lake in the boreal forest of northern Quebec, no snow. The upper half of the image is a huge calm empty sky in plain off-white paper, left empty for a title, no sun, no clouds. Low rounded dark forested hills on the far shore, below the middle of the image. Calm water with carved ripple lines and a band of orange light. On the near shore on the left, a small log sauna with its door ajar and warm orange light inside, a soft puff of steam rising from the roof; a wooden dock running out into the water; three white birch trees with dark marks on the right; tall grass and rocks on the shore. Folk art printmaking, soulful, warm, handmade, edge to edge with no margin. No text, no letters, no signature, no border, no people, no animals, no boat, no mountains.
