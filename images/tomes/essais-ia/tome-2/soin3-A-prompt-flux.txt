Vertical linocut print, hand-carved relief print with bold white gouge cuts carved into the dark shapes, carved outlines and hatching, visible carving marks and paper grain, printed full bleed with only three inks: very dark oxblood maroon, off-white paper and warm orange. A quiet still life inside a log cabin in the evening, the corner where people are cared for. The upper half of the image is a plain dark oxblood wall, one flat area of ink, calm and empty. Below, on a table with a white linen cloth beside a black cast iron wood stove with orange fire behind its open door and a black kettle with a little steam on top: an oil lamp with a glass chimney and a small orange flame, two glass jars of dried herbs, a wooden mortar and pestle, a white enamel basin of hot water with a little steam, two rolled cotton bandages, a sprig of balsam fir and a sprig of Labrador tea lying on the cloth. Nothing else. Folk art printmaking, soulful, warm, handmade, edge to edge with no margin. No text, no letters, no labels, no signature, no border, no people, no hands, no animals.
