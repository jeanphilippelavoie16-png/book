Vertical linocut print, hand-carved relief print with visible gouge marks, only three inks: deep oxblood red (very dark burgundy brown), off-white paper and warm orange. Inside a log cabin on a winter night. The upper half is a calm, almost empty dark oxblood wall of horizontal logs with carved grain, left empty for a title, one small square window with a few stars. In the lower half: a small cast iron wood stove on short legs, its door ajar with warm orange fire glowing inside and orange light on the floor boards, a kettle on top with white steam curling up, an enamel basin of hot water with a folded cloth on a wooden stool beside the stove, a bundle of balsam fir branches and a steaming mug on a shelf, firewood stacked on the floor. Folk art printmaking, quiet, warm, soulful, handmade, full bleed to the edges. No text, no letters, no signature, no border, no people, no animals.
