Vertical linocut print, hand-carved relief print with visible gouge marks and paper grain, printed full bleed with only three inks: very dark oxblood maroon, off-white paper and warm orange. A quiet still life inside a log cabin in the evening, the corner where people are cared for. The upper half of the image is one plain flat area of dark oxblood maroon, a wall in shadow with no planks, no window and no pattern, completely empty for a title. In the lower half, on a wooden table covered with a white linen cloth beside a black cast iron wood stove: an oil lamp with a glass chimney and a small orange flame casting warm light, three glass jars of dried herbs, a white enamel basin of hot water with a wisp of steam, a folded white towel, sprigs of balsam fir and Labrador tea lying on the cloth, a black kettle with a puff of steam on the stove. Folk art printmaking, soulful, warm, handmade, edge to edge with no margin. No text, no letters, no labels, no captions, no signature, no border, no frame, no people, no animals.
