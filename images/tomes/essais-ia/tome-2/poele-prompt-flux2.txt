Vertical linocut print, hand-carved relief print with visible gouge marks, printed full bleed with only three inks: very dark oxblood maroon (almost black), off-white paper and warm orange. A quiet corner inside a log cabin on a winter night. The upper half of the image is one plain, smooth, flat area of very dark oxblood maroon, a dark wall in shadow with no planks and no pattern, completely empty for a title. In the lower half: a small black cast iron wood stove on short legs, its door ajar with warm orange fire inside and a soft orange glow on the floor, a kettle on top with a white wisp of steam, an enamel basin of hot water with a folded cloth on a wooden stool beside the stove, a sprig of balsam fir and a steaming mug on a small table. Folk art printmaking, soulful, warm, handmade, edge to edge with no margin. No text, no letters, no captions, no signature, no border, no frame, no people, no animals.
