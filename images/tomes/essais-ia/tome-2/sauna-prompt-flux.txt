Vertical linocut print, hand-carved relief print with visible gouge marks, only three inks: deep oxblood red (very dark burgundy brown), off-white paper and warm orange. A winter evening at the edge of a frozen lake in the boreal forest of northern Quebec. The upper half is a huge calm open sky in flat dark oxblood red, almost empty, a few small carved stars, left empty for a title. Low rounded forested hills only at the horizon, below the middle of the image, their crests touched by orange light. A wide frozen white lake, a far shore of tiny dark spruces with a small log cabin and two glowing orange windows. Lower third: a small log smoke sauna on the lakeshore, its door half open with warm orange firelight inside spilling onto the snow, white steam and smoke curling up from the roof, deep snow with carved drift lines, a footpath, tall slender dark spruces heavy with snow framing both sides. Folk art printmaking, soulful, handmade, full bleed to the edges. No text, no letters, no signature, no border, no people, no animals.
