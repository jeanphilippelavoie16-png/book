Vertical linocut print, hand-carved relief print with bold white gouge cuts carved into the dark shapes, carved outlines and hatching, visible carving marks and paper grain, printed full bleed with only three inks: very dark oxblood maroon, off-white paper and warm orange. A quiet still life inside a log cabin in the evening, the corner where people are cared for. The upper half of the image is a plain off-white paper wall, calm and empty, with the objects printed in dark oxblood ink. Wide view: a dark wooden table on the left with two glass jars of dried herbs, a wooden mortar and pestle, a white enamel basin of hot water with a little steam and two rolled cotton bandages; on the right a black cast iron wood stove with orange fire behind its open door and a black kettle with a little steam on top. Nothing else. Folk art printmaking, soulful, warm, handmade, edge to edge with no margin. No text, no letters, no labels, no signature, no border, no people, no hands, no animals.
