Vertical linocut print, hand-carved relief print with bold white gouge cuts carved into the dark shapes, carved outlines and hatching, visible carving marks and paper grain, printed full bleed with only three inks: very dark oxblood maroon, off-white paper and warm orange. A quiet still life inside a log cabin in the evening, the corner where people are cared for. The upper half of the image is a plain brick-red oxblood wall, one flat area of ink, calm and empty. Wide view: a black cast iron wood stove in the middle, orange fire behind its open door, a black kettle with a little steam on top, orange light on the floor boards; on the left a wooden stool with a white enamel basin of hot water with a little steam; on the right a folded towel, two rolled cotton bandages and a small stack of split firewood. Nothing else. Folk art printmaking, soulful, warm, handmade, edge to edge with no margin. No text, no letters, no labels, no signature, no border, no people, no hands, no animals.
