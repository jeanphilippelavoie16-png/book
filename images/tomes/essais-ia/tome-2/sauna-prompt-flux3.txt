Vertical linocut print, hand-carved relief print with visible gouge marks and paper grain, printed full bleed with only three inks: a very dark oxblood maroon that is almost black, off-white paper, and small touches of warm orange. Winter night at the edge of a frozen lake in the boreal forest of northern Quebec. The upper half of the image is a huge calm night sky printed as one flat solid area of very dark oxblood maroon with a few tiny carved stars and a small pale moon low on the left, completely empty for a title, no gradient, no sunset. Low rounded dark hills only at the horizon, below the middle of the image. A wide frozen lake of white snow; on the far shore, among tiny dark spruces, a small cabin with two orange windows. In the foreground on the left, a small low windowless log sauna seen from the front, its wooden door wide open toward us with warm orange firelight inside and a soft orange glow on the snow at the doorstep, white steam billowing out of the open door and from under the roof and drifting up a little. Firewood stacked beside it. White snow with carved drift lines and a few snowshoe tracks, tall slender dark spruces heavy with snow framing both sides. Folk art printmaking, soulful, handmade, edge to edge. No text, no letters, no signature, no monogram, no border, no margin, no people, no animals, no mountains.
