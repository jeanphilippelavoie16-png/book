Full-bleed book cover illustration in the style of a hand-carved linocut: rough gouge marks and carving texture in every flat area, paper grain, bold hand-cut shapes with slightly irregular edges, Quebec folk art. The dark ink runs off all four edges, no paper border, no margin, unsigned. Only three colours: very dark slate blue, off-white, warm orange. A summer evening on the shore of a calm lake in the boreal forest of the Quebec north shore. No snow anywhere. The upper half is a plain, calm, empty dark slate blue sky with a few tiny stars; the round sun is setting behind the low forested hills of the far shore, flat orange, and its reflection on the calm lake is a column of short carved orange strokes. On the near shore, dark ground with tall grass and wild plants carved in off-white strokes, a few rocks: a wooden drying rack of two forked posts and a crossbar with whole trout hanging, a small campfire with orange flames and a thin wisp of smoke drifting toward the fish, one small log cabin with a glowing orange window under black spruces on the right; tall black spruces framing the right side. Soulful, quiet and warm. No text, no letters, no signature, no snow, no boats, no people, no animals, no blood.
