Vertical linocut print, hand-carved relief print with visible gouge marks and carving texture, printmaking paper grain, full bleed with no border and no margin. Only three inks: very dark slate blue, off-white paper, warm orange. Dusk at the end of autumn on the shore of a calm lake in the boreal forest of the Quebec north shore, the first snow on the ground. The upper two fifths of the image is a calm, almost empty dark slate blue sky with a few tiny carved stars, left empty for a title. Low rounded forested hills on the far shore only, below the middle of the image. In the lower half, on the near shore: a wooden drying rack of thin poles with rows of split trout hanging above a small smoky fire with glowing orange embers; next to it a small wooden plank smokehouse with smoke curling from its roof and a warm orange glow at its door; an overturned canoe in the snow; across the bay, a small log cabin with one glowing orange window. Tall slender black spruces with snow frame both sides, carved ripple lines on the water. Folk art printmaking, soulful, quiet and warm. No text, no letters, no signature, no people, no animals, no blood.
