Full-bleed book cover illustration in the style of a hand-carved linocut: rough gouge marks and carving texture in every flat area, paper grain, bold hand-cut shapes with slightly irregular edges, Quebec folk art. The dark ink runs off all four edges, no paper border, no margin, unsigned. Only three colours: very dark slate blue, off-white, warm orange. An evening at the end of autumn in the boreal forest of the Quebec north shore, the first snow on the ground. The upper half is a plain, calm, empty dark slate blue sky with a thin crescent moon and a few tiny stars. Low rounded forested hills near the horizon. In the snow below: a small plank smokehouse with a thick white plume of smoke curling from its roof and a warm orange glow at its half open door; a wooden pole between two snow-laden black spruces with three large meat quarters wrapped in white cloth bags hanging in front of a dark spruce thicket; a small plank door set into a low snowy mound, the root cellar; one small log cabin further away with a glowing orange window and chimney smoke; tall snow-laden black spruces framing both sides. Soulful, quiet and warm. No text, no letters, no signature, no people, no animals, no blood.
