Full-bleed book cover illustration in the style of a hand-carved linocut: rough gouge marks and carving texture in every flat area, paper grain, bold hand-cut shapes with slightly irregular edges, Quebec folk art. The dark ink runs off all four edges, no paper border, no margin, unsigned. Only three colours: very dark slate blue, off-white, warm orange. Dusk at the end of autumn on the shore of a calm lake in the boreal forest of the Quebec north shore, the first snow on the ground. The upper half is a plain, calm, empty dark slate blue sky with a few tiny stars, warming to a band of orange sunset light just above the low forested hills of the far shore; the setting sun is reflected on the lake in carved orange lines. On the near shore: a wooden drying rack of two forked posts and two crossbars with rows of small whole trout hanging head down, above a campfire with orange flames and a curl of white smoke; one canoe turned upside down on the snow; one small log cabin with a glowing orange window under snow-laden black spruces on the right; tall black spruces framing both sides. Soulful, quiet and warm. No text, no letters, no signature, no boats on the water, no people, no animals, no blood.
