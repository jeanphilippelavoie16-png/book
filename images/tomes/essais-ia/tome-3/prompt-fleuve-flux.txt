Vertical linocut print, hand-carved relief print with visible gouge marks, only three inks: very dark forest green, off-white cream paper and warm orange. Full bleed, the print fills the whole image edge to edge, no border, no margin. Autumn dawn on the salt-water estuary of the St. Lawrence river on the Côte-Nord of Quebec, so wide it looks like the sea. The upper half is a huge calm empty sky in solid dark forest green with only a few carved horizontal lines low near the horizon, calm and empty for a title, no stars. The sun just rising over the flat horizon of the water, with carved orange rings and a long orange path of light on the water. A low flight of Canada geese in a V just above the water line. Far away a thin low shore. In the middle ground a rocky point with black spruces and golden tamaracks, a harbour seal resting on a rock, and a traditional fish weir: a line of wooden stakes standing in the tidal water. Lower third: a pale pebble beach at low tide with seaweed, one small wooden rowboat pulled up on the beach. Folk art printmaking, soulful, handmade. No text, no letters, no signature, no people.
