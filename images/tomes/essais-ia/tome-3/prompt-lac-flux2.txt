Vertical linocut print, hand-carved relief print with visible gouge marks and carving texture, printmaking paper grain, only three inks: very dark forest green, off-white cream paper and warm orange. Full bleed, the print fills the whole image edge to edge, no border, no margin. Autumn dawn on a lake in the boreal forest of the Côte-Nord, Quebec. The upper half of the image is a huge calm empty sky printed as a flat block of dark forest green ink with faint carving marks, completely empty: no birds, no clouds, no stars. Near the horizon a few carved horizontal lines of cream and orange. The sun just rising, half hidden behind low rounded forested hills at the horizon, below the middle of the image, with an orange reflection on still water and mist lying in bands on the lake. On the far shore a dense row of black spruces with golden orange tamaracks. Alone in the shallow water near the far shore, a bull moose with broad palmate antlers, small in the distance, with its reflection. Lower third: a pebble beach with one empty birch-bark canoe pulled up on the shore, a paddle leaning against it, tall slender black spruces framing both sides and one golden tamarack. Folk art printmaking, soulful, handmade. No text, no letters, no signature, no people.
