Vertical linocut print, hand-carved relief print with visible gouge marks, only three inks: very dark forest green, off-white cream paper and warm orange. Full bleed, the print fills the whole image edge to edge, no border, no margin. Autumn dawn on a lake in the boreal forest of the Côte-Nord, Quebec. The upper half is a huge calm empty sky in solid dark forest green with only a few carved horizontal lines low near the horizon, calm and empty for a title, no stars. The sun just rising, half hidden behind low rounded forested hills at the horizon, below the middle of the image, with carved orange rings and an orange reflection on still water. Mist lying in bands on the lake. On the far shore a row of black spruces with golden orange tamaracks; one bull moose with broad palmate antlers standing in the shallows, small in the distance. Lower third: a pebble beach with one birch-bark canoe pulled up on the shore, a paddle leaning against it, tall slender black spruces framing both sides and one golden tamarack. Folk art printmaking, soulful, handmade. No text, no letters, no signature, no people.
