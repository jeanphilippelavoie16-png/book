Vertical linocut print, hand-carved relief print with visible gouge marks and carving texture, printmaking paper grain, only three inks: very dark forest green, off-white cream paper and warm orange. Full bleed, the print fills the whole image edge to edge, no border, no margin. Autumn dawn on a wide lake in the boreal forest of the Côte-Nord, Quebec. The upper half of the image is a huge calm empty sky printed as a flat block of very dark forest green ink, completely empty: no birds, no clouds, no stars. Near the horizon a few carved horizontal lines of cream and orange. The sun just rising behind low rounded forested hills, below the middle of the image, with an orange reflection on still water and bands of mist on the lake. Far away on the opposite shore, very small, the dark silhouette of a moose standing at the water's edge under black spruces and golden orange tamaracks. In the foreground, on a pale sandy beach, an empty birch-bark canoe lies pulled up on the sand, a paddle resting across it; tall slender black spruces frame both sides. The bottom of the image is pale sand with carved lines. Folk art printmaking, soulful, handmade. No text, no letters, no signature, no people.
