Full-bleed vertical linocut print, rough hand-carved relief print: every shape cut with a gouge, visible gouge strokes and chisel marks in the sky, in the snow and on the walls, uneven inked edges, slight ink speckle, textured printmaking paper, printed edge to edge. Only three inks: very dark warm charcoal grey, off-white paper and warm orange. Winter evening in the boreal forest. A calm, nearly empty dark charcoal sky over the upper half with a few small carved stars and a thin crescent moon. Low rounded hills carved with fine parallel white lines, a band of small black spruces at the horizon. A plank workshop shed with a snowy gable roof, its wide double door open on the orange glow of a small blacksmith forge, an anvil on a log stump in silhouette, tongs hanging on the back wall, orange sparks, smoke curling from the stovepipe, carved orange light on the snow, a small log cabin with a lit window, a woodpile, an axe in a chopping block, snow-laden black spruces carved with white lines on both sides. Folk art printmaking, soulful, handmade, like a woodcut by a northern artist. No text, no letters, no signature, no initials, no animals.
