Full-bleed vertical linocut print, hand-carved relief print with visible gouge marks and paper grain, printed edge to edge with no paper margin and no border, only three inks: very dark warm charcoal grey, off-white paper and warm orange. Low rounded forested hills only at the horizon, below the middle of the image, no mountains. Lower half: deep snow with carved drift lines, a small plank workshop shed with a snowy gable roof and its large double door wide open, inside the orange glow of a small blacksmith forge, orange sparks drifting out of the door, an anvil on a log stump, saws and tools hanging on the inside wall, smoke curling from a stovepipe, a warm orange pool of light on the snow, a small log cabin with one glowing window behind, a woodpile, tall snow-laden black spruces framing both sides. Folk art printmaking, soulful, handmade. No text, no letters, no signature, no initials, no people.
