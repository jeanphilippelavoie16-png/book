--- Texte explicatif de la page
These printing considerations apply to your cover PDFs and your page PDFs: Bleed — The bleed measurement when added to the trim size measurement will equal the final size of the PDFs that are required for successful upload and printing with Blurb. Any imagery or color that you would like to bleed off of the edge of your book pages or cover should be extended out past the trim by this bleed amount. Any text or images that appear in your design between the trim line and the bleed line will not actually print in your final book. Please note, that while bleed is added to all four sides of your covers, it is only added to the top, bottom, and outside of your individual page layout. The inside edge is the gutter of your book. Trim — Trim size indicates where the cover or pages end and where the paper is cut to your specified book size. This is the finished size of your book after the bleed area has been trimmed. On the ImageWrap book, the trim line references where the image will be wrapped around the edge of the cover. Safe text boundary — The safe text area is inset from the trim line by 18 pts (.25 in, 0.6 cm) on all outside edges and 45 pts (.625 in, 1.587 cm) for the gutter. This indicates where you can insert text and images and be sure that they will not be trimmed too close to the edge or bound into the gutter. Any and all critical content, like text, should be kept within the safe area. It is similar to defining a margin in word processing documents
